**Frozen position:** rank 7 of 24; score 56.4/100; confidence medium; TRL 3; US beachhead; China is not countable. G2, G3, and G5 pass. G1, G4, G6, and G7 remain pass-marginal. ## Thesis

DC protection is a real systems bottleneck because a DC fault rises rapidly and does not offer the natural current zero crossing that simplifies AC interruption. In a multi-terminal network, a protection system must detect, classify, locate, and isolate the fault quickly enough to protect converters and preserve healthy sections, yet selectively enough to avoid turning every local event into a full-link shutdown. Recent peer-reviewed papers continue to propose different wavelet, traveling-wave, non-unit, and coordination methods rather than pointing to a single settled scheme ([L08-004](https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1073/18/5/1147), [L08-005](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-19589-0), [L08-006](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/rpg2.12903)). A 2026 study compares six wavelets across three- and four-terminal grids and includes HIL validation, demonstrating both technical feasibility and continuing method fragmentation ([L08-007](https://doi.org/10.3390/app16115186)).

The investable wedge is therefore not merely another clever trip algorithm. It is a vendor-neutral evidence layer: a reproducible fault library, interface adapters, model-version controls, and a qualification report that lets a developer, owner, engineering contractor, or converter supplier compare protection behavior before field commissioning. A reference relay implements the benchmark and proves that the test is not purely academic. Over time, the benchmark could become a procurement specification and the relay could become a merchant product for projects willing to separate protection from the converter package.

Integrated converter suppliers already own protection hardware, station models, and commissioning relationships. The thesis survives only if a US owner values independent evidence enough to require supplier participation. The frozen G4 pass-marginal and raw 10x score of one are appropriate: no order-of-magnitude edge has been demonstrated.

## Exact buyer, job, and demand evidence

The first buyer is not a generic utility. It is the protection or engineering authority responsible for accepting a specific DC link, multi-terminal demonstration, or MVDC distribution system. Candidate economic buyers include a transmission developer's engineering director, an owner's engineer, a converter supplier's validation organization, and a shared test laboratory working under a project contract.

The exact job is: before energization, prove that the combined converter–relay–breaker system clears an enumerated set of internal faults within its damage envelope, rejects external faults, remains selective under sensor and communication failures, and produces a traceable record that survives design changes. The product must also rerun the suite when firmware, network topology, current limit, or protection settings change. For MVDC distribution, the analogous job is preventing a branch fault from cascading across a power-dense facility. Peer-reviewed work names millisecond-level self-healing for smart DC distribution and explicitly situates the problem in datacenter power distribution ([L08-017](https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1073/18/14/3613)). Arc-detection research shows that other DC fault classes remain active signal-processing problems, although PV arc detection is adjacent evidence and must not be presented as proof of HVDC demand ([L08-019](https://www.mdpi.com/2571-6255/9/2/84)).

US demand evidence is substantial at the host-system level but incomplete at the product level. Pattern Energy's Southern Spirit page describes a roughly 320-mile, 3,000 MW, ±525 kV VSC-HVDC interconnector and a multibillion-dollar project supported by the DOE Transmission Facilitation Program ([L08-041](https://patternenergy.com/projects/southern-spirit-transmission/)). Invenergy announced nearly $1.7 billion of contractor awards for the 800-mile Grain Belt Express and described it as connecting four grid regions ([P3R2-E-14-S01](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/grain-belt-express-awards-1-7b-to-us-contractors-quanta-and-kiewit-to-build-largest-transmission-line-in-us-history---invenergy-302448664.html)). Grid United lists four additional interregional projects, including the up-to-525 kV North Plains Connector and Three Corners Connector ([P3R2-E-14-S02](https://gridunited.com/projects/)). These are named, capital-intensive programs with protection work. They do **not** prove that each DC topology is meshed, that each requires the proposed discrimination method, or that any buyer will unbundle protection from the converter supplier.

GE Vernova's reported roughly $10 billion HVDC backlog supports the equipment cycle; its datacenter orders are only adjacent evidence for an MVDC expansion ([L08-033](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/04/22/ge-vernova-gev-q1-2026-earnings-transcript/)). FERC Order 1920 adds long-term planning and interregional coordination requirements ([P3R2-E-14-S03](https://rmi.org/insight/understanding-fercs-order-1920/)). PRC-029-1 shows active protection regulation but is not a DC-relay purchase mandate ([L08-043](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/07/29/2025-14304/reliability-standards-for-frequency-and-voltage-protection-settings-and-ride-through-for)). G1 remains marginal until an owner, engineer, or OEM buys a neutral study or specifies the benchmark.

## Product boundary

Version one is a lab product and engineering service with five bounded components:

1. A parameterized real-time network model for four-terminal DC fault studies, with controlled converter, cable, breaker, sensor, and communication variants.
2. A pre-registered fault and nuisance-event library covering internal and external faults, fault resistance, inception point, power-flow direction, noise, missing samples, time synchronization errors, breaker delay, and model mismatch.
3. Interface adapters that connect simulated currents and voltages to a reference relay and ingest trip, block, alarm, and diagnostic outputs from candidate devices.
4. A deterministic scoring harness for detection time, selectivity, security, dependability, recovery, and repeatability, including raw-data hashes and model-version provenance.
5. A qualification dossier that states the tested envelope and failures without claiming universal system certification.

The reference relay is a hardware implementation of selected traveling-wave or non-unit methods, not a breaker, converter controller, or station automation system. The company does not design the HVDC power stage, guarantee grid stability, certify a project on behalf of a regulator, or provide 24/7 operational protection before it has the staff and insurance to do so. Proprietary OEM-model access and confidentiality must be contractually solved.

Begin with fixed-scope studies and annual benchmark licenses. Offer relay hardware only after a buyer requests an independent device. This tests whether owners pay for neutral evidence despite bundled OEM protection.

## Physics and build path

The measurement chain begins with voltage and current transients created when a fault launches waves along a DC cable or line. Traveling-wave methods use arrival time, polarity, and high-frequency features; non-unit methods seek decisions without a synchronized measurement at both ends; wavelet methods extract transient energy across time and scale. Every approach encounters practical ambiguities: high-impedance faults reduce signal strength, reflections depend on topology and terminations, converters change the transient, noise and sensor bandwidth distort features, and communication-assisted schemes add delay or loss. The literature's variety is a warning against selecting an algorithm on a clean simulation alone ([L08-004](https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1073/18/5/1147), [L08-005](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-19589-0)).

Protection is also coupled to interruption hardware. Research on half-bridge modular multilevel converter grids emphasizes coordination between the relay logic and DC protection devices ([L08-006](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/rpg2.12903)). Multiport hybrid-breaker research seeks lower-cost network architectures ([L08-001](https://www.sciopen.com/article/10.17775/CSEEJPES.2021.05040)), while a fault-current-limiter topology is proposed to reduce electrical and thermal stress on hybrid breakers ([L08-003](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-27261-w)). The startup must therefore report an operating envelope—fault current, breaker opening delay, converter blocking behavior—not a context-free “less than one millisecond” number.

The build path has four stages. First, reproduce three peer-reviewed algorithms on the same offline model and audit whether published metrics survive common definitions. Second, move the model into a real-time simulator and inject measurement noise, bandwidth, timestamp, and communication faults. Third, connect an FPGA/DSP reference relay through analog or digital I/O and measure wall-clock decisions, jitter, false trips, and reset behavior. Fourth, test a sealed third-party device or controller only after a signed model-use, data-rights, and laboratory-access agreement. Each stage ends with immutable test vectors and a discrepancy log.

Sub-millisecond latency is a target, not the 10x thesis. The scorecard must include worst-case latency, missed and false trips, zone selectivity, robustness to high resistance and topology change, communication-fault recovery, and reproducibility.

## Incumbents, strongest bear case, and 10x test

The strongest bear case is that the project exists but the company does not. Converter OEMs can bundle protection with their own controls, proprietary models, factory tests, commissioning teams, and long warranties. NR Electric openly lists LCC-HVDC and VSC-HVDC control and protection systems plus high-voltage DC breakers, demonstrating the breadth of an integrated incumbent platform ([P3R2-E-14-S04](https://www.nrec.com/en/)). The accepted packet also identifies GE Vernova's backlog as evidence of well-capitalized incumbent execution ([L08-033](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/04/22/ge-vernova-gev-q1-2026-earnings-transcript/)). Other integrated suppliers and established relay companies can extend their products, while project consortia can create common test methods without a startup.

Neutrality matters only if it changes acceptance. Without credible models and devices, the benchmark is an academic artifact OEMs can ignore. Buyers may still prefer a recognized institute, owner's engineer, or converter supplier. Service requires strict confidentiality, model control, cybersecurity, witnessing, liability coverage, and long-lived support.

No 10x advantage is currently proven. The decisive 10x test is workflow-level: compared with the buyer's present multi-vendor protection study, can the platform cut the elapsed time to rerun a pre-agreed regression suite after a settings or model change by at least 10x while reproducing the incumbent study's trip/no-trip conclusions on at least 99% of cases? The denominator includes model import, interface mapping, test execution, exception review, and report generation—not only simulator runtime. A second condition is that the neutral suite reveals at least one consequential interoperability or selectivity defect that the ordinary single-vendor procedure misses. If the platform is merely faster at running an untrusted model, the advantage is not buyer-valued.

## Decisive experiment, budget, access condition, and kill rules

The frozen $300,000 experiment benchmarks three published algorithms on a four-terminal real-time model and demonstrates a reference relay with less than one millisecond decision latency. A planning budget is: $85,000 for time on an RTDS-class system, I/O, and lab support; $45,000 for FPGA/DSP relay hardware, analog conditioning, time synchronization, and enclosure; $60,000 for two graduate researchers or equivalent engineering labor; $30,000 for independent protection review; $25,000 for fault-library and model development; $20,000 for safety, cybersecurity, and data infrastructure; $15,000 for publication and reproducibility packaging; and $20,000 contingency. These are assumptions, not quotations.

The access condition is explicit: before committing the full budget, obtain a signed laboratory agreement that reserves adequate real-time simulator capacity and permits publication of the generic benchmark. No OEM cubicle, proprietary model, utility data, purchase order, or customer access is assumed. If simulator access is not signed, spend only a bounded offline-model phase and stop before buying the reference hardware. A later commercial validation requires a separate signed agreement covering the candidate device, model rights, witness procedure, confidentiality, and the customer's right to use the report.

Technical pass thresholds are pre-registered: all three algorithms reproduced against their stated baseline; the reference relay completes the trip decision in under 1 ms for at least 99.9% of in-envelope internal-fault runs; false trips remain below 0.1% on the declared external-fault and nuisance set; selectivity is preserved across predeclared fault resistance, power-flow, noise, sensor, and communication cases; and an independent reviewer can rerun the suite from the released artifacts. Passing latency while failing security or reproducibility is a failure.

Kill or materially narrow the thesis if the benchmark cannot reproduce published results; no method maintains the pass thresholds across realistic model variation; I/O and clock uncertainty consume the claimed latency margin; or the results depend on undisclosed manual tuning. The commercial wedge is killed if, by end-2029, no US owner, owner's engineer, shared laboratory, or OEM will sign a paid evaluation path with device/model access and acceptance criteria. A technically excellent open benchmark without governed access is a research contribution, not a company.

## Bottom-up niche arithmetic

Use a project-and-labor model because public relay pricing is absent. The observable US pipeline in the packet contains Southern Spirit, Grain Belt Express, and four Grid United projects: six development programs, not six confirmed multi-terminal relay opportunities. Assume only two to four reach a protection-study stage relevant to the neutral platform by 2034. Assume each relevant program buys one initial conformance study at $0.4–1.2 million and one to three reruns or change studies at $0.15–0.5 million. That yields roughly $1.1–10.8 million from the named program set. All rates and conversion counts are assumptions.

Add three to six reachable converter, engineering, or laboratory organizations, each paying $0.2–0.6 million annually for tools, updates, and support after qualification. At 50% penetration, that is roughly $0.3–1.8 million of annual recurring revenue. Five to twenty reference-relay or interface systems at an assumed $0.15–0.4 million each add $0.75–8 million over several years. The combined early niche can support a focused engineering company, but it does not independently establish venture scale. Expansion to MVDC facilities, offshore meshes, recurring compliance regression, or a broadly adopted test specification is required for a much larger outcome.

The arithmetic is fragile in both directions. One mandated neutral test regime could expand the market; OEM refusal could collapse it. A project delay can move revenue by years. Liability and senior protection-engineering labor can also erase attractive gross margins. The correct precompany objective is not maximizing a speculative market size; it is proving that a buyer will pay for a repeatable neutral decision artifact.

## US route

The US route starts with simulator access and interviews, which are discovery rather than demand. After the 2027 experiment, seek two written evaluations of the report format and one governed device study in 2028. By 2029 require a paid study, specification insertion, or funded qualification plan with acceptance criteria.

Southern Spirit and Grain Belt are dated anchors; Grid United diversifies the pipeline. Reporting on Southern Spirit documents legislative and incumbent challenges that can move the revenue date ([P3R2-E-14-S06](https://grist.org/energy/southern-spirit-transmission-line-louisiana-mississippi-texas/)). The route should survive one delay but is killed if all relevant projects remain point-to-point, integrated OEM scope through 2034.

Sell “independent, reproducible evidence before commissioning,” not OEM replacement. Exclude operational trip authority from the first contract; offer a pilot relay only after two witnessed studies agree with buyer baselines.

China is explicitly non-countable for this idea. A 2026 State Grid tender award to XJ Electric included RMB 104 million of DC control and protection systems within a larger DC-transmission award, proving domestic procurement of the category ([L08-034](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-05-07/4382365.html)). Those records validate the technical job while weakening the merchant-entry case.

No China entity, license revenue, partner, technology transfer, or market access is assumed. The base plan does not require China sales. Any future discussion would require current restricted-party, end-use, export-classification, cybersecurity, source-code, model-data, and local-liability review before technical disclosure. Until then, China evidence is competitor and category intelligence only, not a beachhead, secondary market, or portfolio quota contribution.

## Safety, compliance, and quality

The lab combines real-time simulation, low-voltage analog signals, timing networks, and potentially relay trip outputs connected to higher-energy test equipment. The first rig should remain power-hardware-free unless a separate safety case is approved. It needs guarded wiring, isolated I/O, emergency stop, documented grounding, current-limited supplies, controlled configuration, and a formal test authorization. Any later power-HIL stage requires fault-energy analysis, arc-flash boundaries, interlocks, and qualified supervision.

Software quality is safety work. Retain model version, parameters, firmware hash, clock, I/O calibration, fault seed, operator actions, and raw waveform. Reports must distinguish simulation from field certification, disclose the envelope, and examine false trips as rigorously as fast trips.

Map the actual buyer specification, interconnection rules, setting governance, and cyber requirements. PRC-029-1 is context, not evidence that this relay is certified ([L08-043](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/07/29/2025-14304/reliability-standards-for-frequency-and-voltage-protection-settings-and-ride-through-for)). Avoid unsupported “compliant” language; require liability coverage, secure model handling, vulnerability response, and reproducibility before launch.

## 2026–2029 milestones and 2030–2034 triggers

**2026:** define the four-terminal reference model, fault taxonomy, metrics, and publication rights; secure signed simulator access; reproduce three algorithms offline. **2027:** complete the real-time benchmark and reference relay; publish generic test vectors, uncertainty sources, and failures. **2028:** demonstrate cross-platform reproducibility, add version-controlled adapters, obtain two buyer-side written evaluations of the dossier, and pursue one governed device study. **2029:** require a paid evaluation, funded qualification plan, or specification insertion. Do not incorporate around an unsigned promise.

**2030–2031:** if the 2029 gate passes, sell project studies while keeping field trip authority outside scope; qualify an interface kit and audit process. Refresh Southern Spirit, Grain Belt, and Grid United topology, construction, OEM, and commissioning schedules every six months. **2032–2034:** expand from one-off studies to regression subscriptions only if project changes create recurring demand. Pilot a merchant relay only where an owner explicitly separates protection from converter scope. Kill the broader platform if there is no multi-terminal or MVDC project with neutral-test procurement by 2033, consistent with the frozen commercial readiness deadline.

The most important trigger is not an in-service date. It is a contracting event that gives the neutral lab governed access to a credible model or device and makes its result part of acceptance. Project awards without that bridge keep G1 and G7 marginal.

## Evidence durability and refresh sensitivity

The durable layer is the peer-reviewed physics: multi-terminal fault detection and discrimination ([L08-004](https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1073/18/5/1147), [L08-005](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-19589-0)); protection coordination ([L08-006](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/rpg2.12903)); comparative HIL validation ([L08-007](https://doi.org/10.3390/app16115186)); DC distribution protection ([L08-017](https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1073/18/14/3613)); breaker topology ([L08-001](https://www.sciopen.com/article/10.17775/CSEEJPES.2021.05040)); and breaker-stress mitigation ([L08-003](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-27261-w)). These sources establish a difficult and testable engineering problem, not product demand.

Refresh-sensitive evidence includes project topology and dates, awards, OEM scope, regulatory deadlines, corporate backlog, incumbent product features, and buyer access. Even the packet's external comparison data—large converter-station spending and localization activity—should be treated as category context rather than a US forecast ([L08-038](https://dealsite.co.kr/articles/150180), [L08-039](https://zdnet.co.kr/view/?no=20260320123044), [P3R2-E-14-S05](https://www.asiae.co.kr/article/2025112515105483577)). Archive primary project pages and refresh them semiannually. No signed access, purchase order, paid study, or customer commitment is claimed here.

## Verdict

**Advance only as a gated precompany benchmark and neutral-lab test.** The opportunity deserves continued work because the protection problem is technically real, the US host-project pipeline is named and funded, and the $300,000 experiment can produce an objective result. It remains a 56.4/100, medium-confidence thesis because host-system spending is not yet product demand, US anchor topology is incompletely verified, integrated OEMs control the interfaces, neutrality is not a proven 10x advantage, and the support and liability burden is substantial.

The next decision is crisp: reproduce three algorithms, meet the combined latency/selectivity/reproducibility thresholds, and secure one governed paid evaluation path in which a US buyer can use the neutral report. If the lab cannot obtain model or device access, if the benchmark adds no consequential finding, or if buyers keep protection entirely inside OEM scope, stop at the research artifact. The “SEL of DC grids” becomes a company only when independent evidence changes an acceptance decision.
