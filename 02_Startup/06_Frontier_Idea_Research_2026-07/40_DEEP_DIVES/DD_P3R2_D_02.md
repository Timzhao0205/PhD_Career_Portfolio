**Frozen position:** rank 1 of 24; score 65.6/100; confidence medium; TRL 4; US and China beachheads. G1, G2, G3, and G5 pass; G4, G6, and G7 remain marginal. The product is a 77 K reel-to-reel instrument that estimates position-resolved critical current and screens for delamination or related defects, then emits a transport-calibrated acceptance dossier. It is not a tape producer, magnet designer, or certification authority.

## Investment thesis

REBCO coated conductor is moving from research-lot material toward kilometer-scale input for high-field magnets and power equipment. The buyer’s problem is not simply whether a reel superconducts. It is whether every consequential segment of a long, expensive reel can be accepted, traced, and compared across production lots before it is buried in a cable or coil. A defect discovered after winding can destroy far more value than the tape segment itself. Peer-reviewed work documents both the scale-up of very-high-current-density wire for a commercial fusion customer and the mechanical failure modes—strain degradation, delamination, and coupled electromagnetic stress—that make quality variation consequential ([L03-003](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-81559-z), [L03-018](https://academic.oup.com/nsr/article/10/3/nwad001/6973212)). A 2025 industry review explicitly connects compact-fusion demand to REBCO production challenges ([L03-019](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2772830725000390)).

The investable wedge is therefore narrower than “superconductor testing.” It is an independent acceptance layer between tape producer and magnet or cable builder: continuous contactless current mapping, a second defect-sensitive modality, transport-current calibration, and a tamper-evident per-meter record. If buyers trust that record, the company can become a neutral referee whose data travels with a reel. The attraction is unusually good precompany falsifiability: a $120,000 test can determine whether the contactless estimate agrees with blinded transport measurements to less than 5% error over a 100 m commercial reel.

The thesis is not that no scanner exists. THEVA’s TAPESTAR is a direct, proven incumbent, offering continuous contactless critical-current scanning with millimeter-scale resolution and an XL configuration for production or incoming inspection ([P3R2-D-02-S01](https://www.theva.com/products/)). That fact validates the purchasing category while sharply limiting the whitespace. The startup only deserves to exist if neutral cross-vendor acceptance, multi-modal defect screening, and independently calibrated reporting create a material advantage over TAPESTAR and producer-built tooling. The frozen G4 pass-marginal is the right posture.

## Exact buyers, job, and current demand proof

The first US buyers are REBCO magnet builders and tape suppliers that must qualify purchased reels before winding. Trade reporting quotes Commonwealth Fusion Systems’ commercial executive on magnet sales to Realta Fusion, an earlier magnet sale to a university experiment, and a technology license to another fusion developer; this is evidence that magnets are already becoming merchant products rather than only internal prototypes ([L03-035](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/02/commonwealth-fusion-systems-leans-on-magnets-for-near-term-revenue/)). A supplier announcement records a several-hundred-kilometer HTS-wire agreement for the ST80-HTS prototype and describes evaluation toward later pilot-plant use ([L03-044](https://www.furukawaelectric.com/en/release/2023/kenkai_20230112.html)). The DOE Milestone-Based Fusion Development Program selected eight companies, including major high-field-magnet programs, and provides an official funding and development backdrop for the same buyer class ([L03-032](https://www.energy.gov/articles/us-department-energy-announces-selectees-107-million-fusion-innovation-research-engine)). These sources do not prove a startup purchase order, but they prove that named organizations are procuring conductor, building magnets, and monetizing magnet systems now.

The exact job is incoming-lot and production-line acceptance: locate low-current segments, distinguish repeatable material variation from sensor or temperature artifacts, flag likely delamination/dropouts, and produce a reel map that lets the buyer decide whether to accept, downgrade, splice, or reject material. The buyer currently relies on a mixture of producer data, contactless scans, sampled transport tests, and bespoke qualification. The category is therefore real, but willingness to pay for an independent dossier—rather than accept the producer’s report—remains unproven.

China has a separate, countable demand leg. Shanghai Superconductor states that it supplied all REBCO tape for a full-scale high-temperature-superconducting central-solenoid prototype, completed batch supply after qualification, that a single coil uses hundreds of kilometers, and that BEST requires more than 1,500 km ([P5-G7CNREQ-S01](https://www.shsctec.com/zh/news/hts-cs-coil-success/)). An official-hosted Xinhua report gives BEST a 2030 objective for net fusion power gain and power-generation demonstration using a compact high-field route ([P5-G7CNREQ-S02](https://www.yidaiyilu.gov.cn/p/0LJSD1S3.html)). Energy Singularity’s primary roadmap separately targets a compact high-field HTS tokamak milestone in 2030 and a demonstration plant with commercial potential in 2035 ([P5-G7CNREQ-S03](https://energysingularity.cn/vision.html)). Those are two independent 2030–2035 host-system triggers tied to exact REBCO consumption, which is why G7 was repaired only to pass-marginal rather than treated as certain.

The China job is not fusion-only. Reporting on the 35 kV, 1.2 km State Grid Shanghai HTS cable documents a commissioned commercial power-cable application ([L03-041](https://www.jiemian.com/article/6938856.html)); a peer-reviewed project account independently corroborates that deployment ([L03-012](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2772830722000072)). An official ITER account also shows that Western Superconducting Technologies completed a decade-long, high-volume qualification and delivery program for other superconducting strands ([L03-031](https://www.iter.org/node/20687/china-completes-its-superconducting-strand-program)). That is not direct REBCO demand, but it demonstrates domestic institutional experience with conductor qualification and traceability.

## Product boundary and business model

The v1 product has four bounded elements: a cryogenic reel transport; a contactless magnetic excitation and Hall-sensor head; a complementary thermal or electromagnetic defect-screening head; and software that converts raw signals into a calibrated per-meter acceptance record. The company sells the scanner, periodic calibration, method qualification, and report-schema licenses. A buyer remains responsible for the final accept/reject decision.

The company does **not** promise to infer full coil lifetime from tape data, certify magnet safety, reveal a customer’s deposition recipe, or sell controlled magnet-design know-how. It does not need to build a fusion magnet, make REBCO, or own a production line. Cable and coil acceptance heads are possible expansions only after tape-level accuracy is demonstrated.

The neutral-data boundary matters commercially. Producer-integrated tools optimize process control; a magnet builder needs independent incoming inspection and comparable evidence across suppliers. The product must segregate each customer’s raw data and models while offering a common measurement schema. “Certified” should initially mean instrument-traceable and method-qualified, not government-approved. The relevant superconductivity standards must be obtained and mapped before any formal certification claim; the current accepted evidence pack contains no fetched standards text.

## Physics and build path

REBCO tape carries shielding or transport current whose magnetic field can be sensed above the tape. A moving excitation/sensor head can generate a spatial response correlated with local current-carrying capacity without making a high-current electrical contact at every point. The inverse problem is not trivial: lift-off, tape width and stack geometry, sensor drift, temperature gradients, scan speed, field history, and angular dependence can all masquerade as current variation. Mechanical literature shows that delamination and strain effects are separate failure channels, so magnetic response alone should not be marketed as a complete defect diagnosis ([L03-018](https://academic.oup.com/nsr/article/10/3/nwad001/6973212)).

The build path is consequently staged. First, scan stationary coupons at 77 K and fit a field-to-transport-current calibration with controlled lift-off and temperature. Second, move to a slow 100 m reel and blind the model team to 50 transport-test results. Third, introduce known geometric, current-dropout, and delamination-like defects to learn which are visible to Hall response and which require thermal excitation. Fourth, raise line speed only after accuracy and repeatability survive reel handling. Fifth, freeze a calibration artifact, uncertainty budget, and data format before a customer-line evaluation.

REBCO’s suitability for next-generation tokamak magnets is supported by a recent peer-reviewed review ([L03-001](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1361-6668/ada9d2)), while an alternative-superconductor analysis is a reminder that the material choice is not immutable ([L03-002](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/pop/article/30/12/120604/2930490/Prospects-of-an-alternative-superconductor)). China’s 35.1 T steady-state fully superconducting magnet, described in a peer-reviewed article and independently by the national research organization, shows that high-field hybrid magnet engineering is advancing ([L03-029](https://www.the-innovation.org/article/id/695a0b800989772a128c5422), [L03-037](https://english.cas.cn/newsroom/cas_media/202509/t20250929_1059028.shtml)). CFETR’s 14.5 T toroidal-field design and prototype-coil work provide another technical demand path, but not a guaranteed procurement schedule ([L03-024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0920379622000631)).

## Named incumbents, bear case, and the explicit 10x test

THEVA/TAPESTAR is the closest incumbent and the most important bear fact. The vendor says the method is already relied on by major tape producers, and its product page describes essentially the core contactless scan. Tape makers can also build proprietary scanners around their own process. A buyer may decide that sampled transport tests plus producer data are sufficient; a producer may refuse to share line access; or the market may standardize around the incumbent before a neutral schema gains adoption. An industry-chain account names multiple scaled China tape producers and deposition approaches, reinforcing that process expertise sits inside well-capitalized manufacturers ([L03-052](https://chinacable.com/news/details/3999)). A lower-quality 2026 market post claims very large Shanghai Superconductor contracts and capacity expansion, but because it is retail commentary rather than a company filing, it should be treated only as a refresh-sensitive lead ([P3R2-D-02-S02](https://caifuhao.eastmoney.com/news/20260522234503785213940?from=guba&name=57K+6L6+6IKh5Lu95ZCn&gubaurl=aHR0cHM6Ly9ndWJhLmVhc3Rtb25leS5jb20vbGlzdCw2MDA1NzdfNi5odG1sP2p1bXBoNT0x)).

No 10x advantage has been proven. The explicit test is: at matched uncertainty and defect recall, can the system characterize at least ten times more tape per operator-hour than a defined sampled transport workflow, while reducing destructive samples by at least 80%? The benchmark must include reel loading, cryogenic stabilization, rescans, data review, and confirmatory transport cuts—not just sensor acquisition speed. Pass requires less than 5% mean absolute error on the blinded current estimate, at least 95% recall for predeclared consequential defects, and 10x end-to-end meters per labor-hour. If TAPESTAR or the buyer’s existing workflow already meets those endpoints within 20% of the proposed system’s total cost, the claimed 10x edge fails and the company should stop or narrow to the independent-dossier service.

## Decisive experiment, budget, and pass/kill rules

The frozen first experiment is a 77 K reel-to-reel Hall-array scan of 100 m of commercial tape, blind-correlated against transport critical-current measurements on 50 cut samples. A plausible $120,000 allocation is: $18,000 for multiple commercial tape lots; $22,000 for cryogenic reel mechanics; $18,000 for the magnetic head and fixtures; $14,000 for sensors, data acquisition, and controls; $20,000 for independent transport measurements; $10,000 for seeded-defect and thermal work; $8,000 for safety and data infrastructure; and $10,000 contingency. These are planning assumptions, not supplier quotes.

Pass if the pre-registered model achieves less than 5% aggregate prediction error, maintains calibration across lots and scan directions, and localizes seeded or naturally occurring low-current regions with a useful uncertainty bound. Kill the core metrology thesis if error exceeds 5% after one documented recalibration cycle, if lift-off or temperature sensitivity makes reel-to-reel repeatability worse than 5%, or if transport testing shows consequential defects that both sensing modalities miss. The 10x throughput gate is a second, independent pass condition before commercial engineering.

No partner access is assumed. Tape can be purchased and transport tests can be outsourced. A production-line pilot is not scheduled until a counterparty signs access, data-rights, and safety terms. Technical success without two written buyer evaluations of the neutral acceptance report by end-2028 is a commercial kill signal.

## Bottom-up niche arithmetic

This is initially a specialist instrumentation market. Use ranges, not a top-down fusion TAM. Assume six reachable tape-production sites across the US and China, with two scanners per site for production and final acceptance: 12 systems. Assume ten magnet, cable, or incoming-inspection sites buy one system each: 10 more. Add four shared laboratory or qualification stations: 26 systems total. At an assumed $0.6–1.2 million selling price, the five-year hardware pool is roughly $16–31 million. At 10–15% annual calibration and service revenue, a fully deployed base yields about $1.6–4.7 million per year; report-schema and analytics licenses could add $0.5–1.5 million if buyers actually share the format.

Every input is uncertain: reachable site count, systems per site, price, replacement cycle, and willingness to buy an independent layer. The arithmetic supports a durable high-end niche, not a standalone billion-dollar outcome. Venture-scale upside requires expansion into cable, joint, and coil acceptance or recurring per-reel data services. Conversely, a profitable focused instrument company remains plausible if only 15–20 systems sell. The capital path—$120,000 decisive test and roughly $1.5–4 million for v1—fits that scale.

## US route

The US route begins with an independent magnet builder’s incoming inspection, not an attempt to displace a tape producer’s process-control tool. In 2026–2027, purchase multiple commercial reels, establish transport-current correlation, and publish an uncertainty budget. In 2028, seek paid evaluation campaigns from two magnet or tape organizations; each campaign must use blinded customer-selected regions and compare against the buyer’s existing method. In 2029, require either a paid pilot placement or a written qualification plan with acceptance endpoints. DOE-backed fusion-company development and merchant magnet sales make this buyer set concrete, but neither implies access or an order ([L03-032](https://www.energy.gov/articles/us-department-energy-announces-selectees-107-million-fusion-innovation-research-engine), [L03-035](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/02/commonwealth-fusion-systems-leans-on-magnets-for-near-term-revenue/)).

The US product should lead with independent incoming-goods evidence, data custody, and avoided winding risk. It should not depend on any one reactor schedule. Kill the US route if no buyer will define a paid acceptance study by end-2029, even if the lab instrument works.

## China route

The China route is separate: locally manufactured, independently developed acceptance instrumentation sold through a properly established local commercial channel to civilian tape producers and qualification facilities. The exact opening is the volume ramp implied by BEST’s stated tape requirement and the 2030/2035 high-field roadmaps, plus established HTS cable operation. The product should contain measurement hardware, calibration artifacts, and acceptance reporting—not magnet design, operating parameters, or sensitive end-use models.

Before any transaction, counsel must perform current official restricted-party, end-use, and technology-control screening for the buyer, beneficial owners, integrator, and final site. No counterparty is treated as cleared in this report. Data and model training must be partitioned between the US and China deployments; customer raw data cannot silently improve a model delivered to the other market. Local serviceability matters because cryogenic mechanics and calibration cannot depend on unrestricted remote access.

The binding commercial gate is one paid production-line evaluation by end-2029 with pre-agreed current-map, defect-escape, and repeatability criteria. Without it, the China leg is killed. The 2030 host timelines are credible enough to test, not certain enough to underwrite inventory.

## Safety, compliance, and quality system

The rig combines cryogens, moving reels, magnetic excitation, electrical instrumentation, and possibly active thermal stimulation. It needs oxygen-deficiency monitoring, ventilation review, pressure relief, guarded pinch points, emergency stops, current and voltage interlocks, magnetic-field boundaries, condensation control, and written recovery procedures. Tape edges and high-tension reels require handling protection. Any laser or high-power excitation added for thermal imaging needs its own enclosure and interlock.

Measurement safety is inseparable from quality. Each scan must preserve calibration version, sensor serials, temperature history, reel tension, lift-off estimate, operator actions, and raw data hashes. A formal uncertainty budget and gauge repeatability/reproducibility study precede acceptance claims. The system should fail closed: if temperature, lift-off, or sensor drift leaves the qualified envelope, it marks the segment unmeasured rather than producing a confident current value.

## Milestones, launch triggers, and kill rules

**2026:** freeze the measurement requirements; obtain the applicable standards; purchase tape lots; build stationary coupon fixtures; interview at least ten buyer-side quality engineers without treating interviews as orders. **2027:** complete the $120,000, 100 m/50-sample blind-correlation experiment and publish the uncertainty budget. **2028:** complete cross-lot repeatability, multi-modal defect studies, and two written buyer evaluations; build a guarded continuous-reel prototype. **2029:** require one paid or contractually funded US evaluation and one paid China production-line evaluation before committing to commercial inventory. **2030:** launch only a qualified scanner with calibration service and an acceptance-data schema.

From 2030–2034, refresh the following triggers every six months: BEST construction and its 2030 objective; Energy Singularity’s 2030/2035 roadmap; US merchant magnet deliveries; DOE program milestones; tape-producer capacity and buyer qualification practices; and TAPESTAR’s capabilities and price. The installed Shanghai cable and the durable physics of REBCO mechanical risk remain useful background, but they do not substitute for current purchase intent.

Company-level kill rules are: less than 5% accuracy is not repeatable; no 10x end-to-end throughput advantage; consequential defect recall below 95%; existing tools match the total workflow within 20% of cost; no paid evaluation in either beachhead by end-2029; buyers reject a neutral report format; applicable standards make the method non-comparable; or compliance constraints prevent a serviceable China product. If only one geography passes, operate as a single-market niche rather than preserving a dual-market story.

## Evidence durability and refresh sensitivity

The most durable evidence is peer-reviewed: commercial-scale high-current REBCO production ([L03-003](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-81559-z)); mechanical and delamination risk ([L03-018](https://academic.oup.com/nsr/article/10/3/nwad001/6973212)); CFETR magnet engineering ([L03-024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0920379622000631)); the 35.1 T result ([L03-029](https://www.the-innovation.org/article/id/695a0b800989772a128c5422)); the Shanghai cable account ([L03-012](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2772830722000072)); and the reviews of REBCO’s fusion role and alternative materials ([L03-001](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1361-6668/ada9d2), [L03-002](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/pop/article/30/12/120604/2930490/Prospects-of-an-alternative-superconductor)). These establish physics and technical relevance, not startup demand.

Refresh-sensitive claims include project dates, funding, company roadmaps, tape quantities, capacity, incumbent specifications, pricing, and restricted-party status. Primary company and official records—BEST, Energy Singularity, Shanghai Superconductor, DOE, and THEVA—should be archived and rechecked. Trade or retail reporting should be corroboration only. No claim of signed access, a purchase order, or current legal clearance is made.

## Verdict

**Advance as a precompany experiment, not yet as a financed equipment launch.** P3R2-D-02 earns its top rank because the painful job is real on both US and China routes, the instrument category is already purchased, the build is aligned with a $120,000 decisive test, and the 2030 timing has exact REBCO-linked triggers. It remains only a 65.6/100, medium-confidence idea because THEVA is a direct incumbent, the independent-referee premium is unproven, the niche is modest, and China execution requires live compliance work and paid local evaluation.

The next decision is crisp: demonstrate less than 5% blinded transport-current error, at least 95% consequential-defect recall, and a true 10x end-to-end throughput advantage, then obtain written paid-evaluation paths in both markets. If any one of those load-bearing conditions fails, the “KLA of superconductors” vision is rhetoric rather than a company.
