# 50_deep — ten deep dives and geography

## Purpose

Write decision-grade deep dives for the selected top ten and a cross-portfolio
geography analysis.

## Inputs allowed

- `outputs/20_p4`
- `outputs/30_redteam`
- `outputs/40_select`
- `outputs/45_packs`
- frozen P0-P3 evidence needed to resolve a claim

Do not read historical deep dives, geography, final portfolio, or audits under
`src/06`.

## Pilot

Draft a short structured prototype for one selected idea and a geography
comparison row for two ideas. Exercise citations, fact/inference/hypothesis
labels, budget/threshold/kill-rule logic, and Windows-safe filenames. Save only
under `pilot/50_deep`; the pilot is not part of the final word count.

## Full outputs under `outputs/50_deep`

- `DEEP`: exactly ten Markdown reports with short Windows-safe names.
- Each report is 2,500-4,000 words and covers thesis, customer job and named
  buyers, product boundary/architecture, feasibility, measurable 10x edge,
  demand, competition, decisive 2026-2029 experiment with budget/thresholds/
  kill rule, 2030-2034 timing, independent US/China paths, optional side
  markets, regulation/safety/export controls, capital/business model, founder
  skill transfer, failure modes, milestones, and source-ID appendix.
- `INDEX.json`: exactly ten `ideas`, each with `idea_id`, `file`,
  `word_count`, `source_ids`, `peer_reviewed_source_ids`, and
  `primary_demand_source_ids`.
- Each report resolves at least 20 sources, at least seven peer-reviewed, and
  at least five primary-demand.
- `GEOGRAPHY.md`: independent US and China paths for all 24, optional side
  markets, and export-control boundaries.
- `ADD_SOURCES.json`: unique normalized array, possibly empty.
- `RESULT.json`: `stage:"50_deep"`, `status:"COMPLETE"`, outputs and checks.

The main Fable agent owns the accepted interpretation and writing.
