# P4 scoring report (2026-07-28)

All 65 frozen longlist ideas were scored independently by the main Fable
5/xhigh agent against the 100-point rubric with hard gates G1-G7. Inputs:
frozen longlist records, the EVIDENCE files (facts and cited sources only —
historical analyst gate opinions treated as non-binding), the source atlas,
and the stage-10 refresh (R10-001..R10-079). Every score above 3 carries cited
source IDs. Weighted points equal score x weight / 5 and reconcile to totals
within 0.25 (one authoring slip on P3R2-D-18 was caught by the arithmetic
self-check and corrected to 66.7 before assembly).

## Calibration

founder transfer lands ~72-82 (D-02, C-01, D-10, C-05). Real-but-contested or

gate failure. The pilot's three fresh scores (C-09 73.7 / A-16 53.8 / F-15
47.9) were not reused; full-pass rescoring produced 72.0, 52.0, and 47.0 —
within calibration drift of the anchors.
