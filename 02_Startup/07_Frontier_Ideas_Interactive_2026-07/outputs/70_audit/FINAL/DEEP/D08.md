# D08 — P3R2-A-10: Closed-loop ion-energy control for plasma etch

## 1. Thesis

Leading-edge etch engineers can now shape the voltage waveform that
drives their plasma, but they still cannot see — much less regulate —
the ion energies that waveform actually produces at the wafer. FACT:
Advanced Energy's Q4-2025 earnings call reports full-year semiconductor
revenue of $840 million, broad acceptance of its EVOS/Everest/NavX
waveform-capable platforms, and the CEO's plain statement that
"customers are encountering problems as they go below two nanometers"
— with the new RF products still only at double-digit-millions,
early-pilot scale [L06-039]. FACT: the peer-reviewed literature has
made tailored-waveform biasing the recognized path to controlling ion
energy distribution functions for atomic-scale processing — sawtooth
and pulse-shaped waveforms for high-aspect-ratio dielectric etch
[L06-009, L06-011], multi-diagnostic ICP demonstrations [L06-010], and
a comprehensive 2026 review of waveform parameters versus ion
flux-energy distributions [L06-027]. FACT: the closest commercial
metrology — Impedans' Semion retarding-field analyzer, the industry
standard for substrate-level ion-energy measurement — is measurement-
only, with adjustment left to the operator; the 2026-07 verification
pass confirmed no closed-loop product exists anywhere
[P3R2-A-10-S04]. INFERENCE: when actuation exists (waveform
generators), sensing exists (RFEA), and process pain is public
(sub-2 nm), the unclaimed step is the loop between them. HYPOTHESIS: a
vendor-neutral retrofit — wafer-level ion-energy metrology fused with
tailored-waveform bias control, holding a commanded IEDF against drift
— becomes the process-control layer of atomic-scale plasma
processing, sold to fabs and OEM process groups rather than bundled
inside any generator vendor's stack.

## 2. Customer job and named buyers

The customer job is to hold an etch process on target at atomic scale:
command a bimodal ion-energy distribution, keep it there within
electron-volts as chamber conditions drift, and correlate that hold to
profile metrics the fab actually ships against — depth, bowing,
selectivity. Named buyers, US leg: the CHIPS-funded leading-edge ramp
— Micron's New York fabs (construction 2026-2028, production ~2030)
and its accelerated Idaho ID2 [R10-005] — whose process groups are the
retrofit's end customers; Advanced Energy's own installed base of
waveform-capable generators as the actuation substrate [L06-039]; and
tool-OEM process organizations, approached as partners rather than
competitors. Named context on the CN leg — demand documented, delivery
restructured: China's RF power market (~$0.97B in 2022, historically
an MKS/Advanced Energy duopoly [L06-054]) is being localized at speed
— Hengyunchang's STAR listing surged 302.8% on the thesis of ending
that duopoly, with Piotech at 63% of its revenue and NAURA, AMEC and
YMTC in its orbit [L06-043, L06-042]; Injet Electric claims 80-90%
domestic substitution on 6/8-inch lines [L06-044, L06-054]; AMEC's
etch business grew 54.7% to RMB 7.3 billion covering 95% of domestic
etch applications [L06-048]. But NAURA and Piotech are BIS
Entity-Listed and AMEC lost Validated End-User status in the December
2024 rule [P3R2-A-10-S01] — so the frozen idea's CN customer list was
structurally undeliverable, and this plan carries the red-team repair:
US-primary, with a CN chapter rebuilt around mature-node scope and
non-listed counterparties behind a hard 2027 counsel gate.

## 3. Product boundary and architecture

The product is a retrofit control layer: (a) wafer-level ion-energy
sensing in the RFEA lineage [P3R2-A-10-S04], engineered for
production-chamber duty rather than laboratory campaigns; (b) a
deterministic control engine that closes the loop from measured IEDF
to tailored-waveform bias commands [L06-027]; (c) drift and
event-detection analytics building on published in-situ abnormal-
discharge detection [L06-018], impedance-based plasma-health
monitoring [L06-025], and OES-derived virtual metrology [L06-024];
and (d) integration adapters for the incumbent actuation ecosystem —
Advanced Energy-class waveform generators [L06-039], Comet's Synertia
real-time RF platform [L06-049, L01-050], TRUMPF [L01-049] and Kyosan
[L06-050] sources. Explicitly outside the boundary: generators
themselves, matching networks (whose control models the literature
supplies [L06-001, L06-002, L06-003]), chucks, and chambers. The moat
is the closed-loop dataset: measured-IEDF-to-profile correlations
accumulated across chambers and recipes — data no generator vendor
sees across its competitors' installations.

## 4. Feasibility

Actuation and sensing are demonstrated; the loop is the venture.
Waveform-to-IEDF physics is modeled and measured across CCP and ICP
regimes [L06-009, L06-011, L06-010]; RFEA sensing is a commercial
product class [P3R2-A-10-S04]; uniformity control via multi-coil
amplitude/phase is published [L06-008]. Three engineering sub-problems
deserve honest treatment. First, production-grade sensing: laboratory
RFEAs interrupt production; the retrofit needs sensor wafers and
in-chamber pickups that survive process chemistry and thermal cycles —
electrostatic-chuck interactions included, for which the JR-chuck
breakdown and plasma-charging literature defines the constraints
[L06-005, L06-006]. Second, loop dynamics: holding a commanded bimodal
IEDF within ±2 eV under drift means actuating through a sheath driven
at radio frequency while sensing at kHz rates — a
system-identification and control problem squarely in the founder's
stack, with matching-network dynamics [L06-001, L06-002] inside the
loop. Third, freedom to operate: the event-detection adjacency is
patent-dense, and the red team made an FTO analysis a standing repair
— scheduled 2027, before productization, alongside the counsel gate.

## 5. Measurable 10x edge

Today's practice is open-loop: set the waveform, run witness wafers,
adjust days later. Closed-loop IEDF hold at ±2 eV against drift is a
step-change on the dimension leading-edge fabs publicly struggle with
[L06-039] — measured directly (IEDF error under seeded drift) and
economically (excursion scrap avoided, qualification time cut). Against
Impedans, the edge is the loop itself — measurement-only versus
regulation [P3R2-A-10-S04]; against generator vendors, the edge is
neutrality plus wafer-level ground truth rather than source-side
inference.

## 7. Competition

Impedans owns vendor-neutral measurement [P3R2-A-10-S04]; the
generator vendors — Advanced Energy [L06-039], MKS, Comet [L06-049,
L01-050], TRUMPF [L01-049], Kyosan [L06-050] — own actuation and could
integrate IEDF inference; Quantemol/Oxford Instruments' tailored-
waveform-biasing commercialization is adjacent R&D moving the same
direction [R10-006]; and China's localization cohort [L06-043,
L06-044] owns its home market. Nobody closes the loop vendor-neutrally
— that is the wedge, and both incumbent classes could move toward it,
which sets the venture's clock.

### 7a. Competitive scenarios and response plan

Scenario A (most likely): generator vendors keep improving source-side
control while Impedans stays measurement-only; the loop stays open
into 2028. Response: standard plan — demo, FTO, fab LOI. Scenario B:
Advanced Energy or MKS ships integrated IEDF inference "good enough"
for most processes. Response: hold the leading edge, where inference
diverges from wafer-level truth at exactly the sub-2 nm nodes in pain
[L06-039]; sell ground-truth verification to the same fabs those
vendors serve — the metrology-versus-inference argument sharpens as
nodes shrink. Scenario C: OEMs lock chamber data behind proprietary
interfaces — the red team's strongest counterargument. Response: enter
at the fab, not the OEM: process groups own their chambers'
qualification and can mandate access; the sensor-wafer route needs no
OEM interface at all; and one OEM partnership (scenario A posture)
converts lockdown from threat to moat. Scenario D: the CN counsel
gate fails in 2027. Response: drop the CN chapter entirely — the
US-primary case stands alone by construction, and the portfolio's
dual-market weight rests on other ideas.

## 8. Decisive 2026-2029 experiment

2027-2028 laboratory campaign: on a CCP/ICP testbed with a waveform-
capable bias chain, close the loop from RFEA-measured IEDF to waveform
commands; hold a commanded bimodal IEDF within ±2 eV under seeded
drift (pressure, chemistry, wall-condition changes); correlate held
versus unheld operation against etch-profile metrics in the virtual-
metrology frame [L06-024]. Budget: $450,000 — above the portfolio
band, honestly, and structured with the sensing integration first so
that a sensing failure costs a fraction. Thresholds: ±2 eV hold across
the drift matrix; loop stability across operating points; measurable
profile-metric improvement attributable to the hold. KILL RULE:
failing the ±2 eV hold under drift, or failing to show correlation to
profile metrics, kills the control claim and folds the venture to
metrology components. Parallel 2027 gates: the FTO analysis on
event detection, and the CN counsel go/no-go. 2028 milestone
(red-team dated repair): one fab process-group evaluation LOI.

## 9. 2030-2034 timing

The waveform-capable installed base builds 2026-2029 [L06-039];
CHIPS-funded fabs reach production 2029-2030 [R10-005]; retrofit
demand — control layers bought for chambers already on the floor —
peaks 2030-2033 as second-generation waveform platforms qualify. A
2030 launch lands the product exactly when the actuation substrate is
ubiquitous and the control gap is quantified by three years of
demonstration data. Adoption 2030-2034: two-plus US fab deployments,
one OEM design-in, and — only if the 2027 gate cleared — a licensed
mature-node CN channel. The clock risk is scenario B: the demo and
LOI milestones are dated early precisely to beat integrated-inference
releases to the leading edge.

One structural feature of this market favors the retrofit route over
waiting for tool-generation turnover: etch chambers live for decades,
and fabs extend platforms across multiple node generations rather than
replacing them. A control layer that installs on the existing fleet
monetizes the whole installed base, not just new tools — which is why
the generator vendors' own retrofit-friendly waveform products ramped
as fast as they did [L06-039], and why the same channel is open to a
metrology-anchored control vendor. The 2030-2033 peak is therefore not
a single procurement window but the front edge of a long upgrade
cycle; missing a quarter costs share, not existence.

## 15. Failure modes

(1) Generator-vendor integrated inference (scenario B) — beaten only
by speed to demonstrated ground truth. (2) OEM data lockdown
(scenario C) — met at the fab and via sensor wafers. (3) CN gate
failure (scenario D) — absorbed by the US-primary structure. (4) FTO
conflict in event detection — surfaced in 2027 before productization.
(5) The loop physics failing at production duty cycles — caught at
$450k by the kill rule.
