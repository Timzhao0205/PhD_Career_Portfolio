# D09 — P3R2-C-09: Standardized solid-state pulsed-power module platform

## 1. Thesis

Every electron-beam sterilizer, cargo scanner, medical linac, and
accelerator RF station is driven by a pulsed-power chain that is still
engineered project-by-project, like power supplies before the brick
converter. FACT: the technical base for modular solid-state pulsed
power is mature and peer-reviewed across two decades — 100 kV/60 A
klystron modulators built from stacked solid-state switches [L05-003],
Marx-generator modulators for medical linacs at the 6 MW class
[L05-004, L05-006], X-band magnetron drivers [L05-005], and
standardized solid-state amplifier module concepts published by
accelerator labs themselves [L05-009]. FACT: the incumbent proof of
market is enormous — ScandiNova has delivered its 3,000th
new-generation pulse modulator to a base of 200+ customers in 50+
countries [P3R2-C-09-S03], and Jema has 18 modulators at the European
Spallation Source alone [L05-030] — yet the incumbents sell
proprietary, closed, complete systems, and the 2026-07 verification
found ScandiNova moving toward more integration (turnkey units with
bundled LLRF [L05-047]), not less. FACT: demand-side procurement is
dated and growing on both legs — Varex's Industrial segment grew 25%
year-on-year on cargo-scanning linacs with $55M+ of orders [L05-033],
and China runs real accelerator tenders from self-shielded cyclotrons
[L05-036] to synchrotron components [L07-045]. INFERENCE: a fragmented,
artisanal supply base under compounding demand is the classic setup
for platformization. HYPOTHESIS: a standardized, stackable solid-state
pulsed-power module family — published open interface, digital droop
control, cross-OEM interoperability — becomes the catalog discipline
of pulsed power: the Vicor brick, applied to megawatt pulses.

## 2. Customer job and named buyers

The customer job is a drive chain that arrives on schedule, meets
droop and jitter specifications out of the box, and can be serviced by
swapping modules rather than shipping a custom cabinet back to its
artisan. Named buyer classes and anchors: beam-system OEMs — the
Varex-class industrial linac builders recapitalizing cargo/NDT fleets
[L05-033]; sterilization-system operators in the IBA industrial
lineage [L05-028]; accelerator laboratories and their module-hungry RF
stations, from ESS's envelope-tracking solid-state amplifiers
[L05-007] to IFMIF-DONES's fully solid-state RF architecture [L05-008]
and Korea's GaN SSPA program [L05-010]; the DOE Accelerator
Stewardship ecosystem (General Atomics, Varian, RadiaBeam among
awardees) as the US program channel [L05-031]; and retrofit programs
like SLAC/CPI-lineage GREEN-RF, which exists precisely because
operating fleets want efficiency upgrades without full replacement
[L05-032]. CN leg: CGN's Dasheng accelerator subsidiary files
half-year reports whose headline order figures remain unverified
pending a text-extractable re-fetch [L05-035] — flagged honestly —
alongside verifiable public tenders (CIRC's three self-shielded
cyclotrons [L05-036]; IHEP's HEPS procurement [L07-045]). Korea:
Vitzro Nextec's ministry-funded klystron localization (80 MW tubes,
~70 units at PAL) marks a side-market ecosystem that buys modulators
to drive what it localizes [L05-037].

## 3. Product boundary and architecture

The product is a module family plus an interface standard: (a)
stackable solid-state switching modules (Marx-cell and
transformer-coupled variants, the topologies of the published lineage
[L05-004, L05-003]) spanning tens of kV to hundreds of kV by
series-parallel composition; (b) digital droop and jitter control per
module with fleet telemetry; (c) a published, open electrical and
control interface so any OEM can integrate, second-source, and service
— the strategic inversion of the incumbents' turnkey direction
[L05-047]; and (d) acceptance protocols built on the 2025 editions of
the governing standards — IEC 60060-1:2025 for high-voltage test
technique [L05-043] and, for sterilization customers, the ISO
11137-1:2025 qualification frame [L05-042]. Explicitly outside the
boundary: klystrons, magnetrons and gyrotrons themselves (the tube
world of Thales-class suppliers [L05-029] and the restructured CPI
electron-device businesses [L05-050]), beam optics, and complete
turnkey systems. The moat is the interface plus the interoperability
dataset — once two OEMs qualify the same module, the category exists
and the first mover owns its definition.

## 4. Feasibility

Nothing here is undemonstrated physics. Solid-state Marx and
modulator topologies are deployed commercially at the 3-100 MW class
[L05-047, P3R2-C-09-S03]; sub-0.1% flatness and microsecond risetimes
are published [L05-003]; the standardized-module concept has
laboratory precedent [L05-009]. Three engineering sub-problems deserve
honest treatment. First, HV packaging at the module boundary:
series-stacking modules to hundreds of kV puts insulation coordination
and corona management at the interface — the one area the P4 scoring
flagged at the founder's profile edge, addressed by hiring HV
packaging depth early and by leaning on the IEC 60060-1:2025 test
frame from day one [L05-043]. Second, droop control across
composition: digital per-module droop compensation must hold 1%-class
flatness when modules stack and loads vary from klystrons [L05-012]
to magnetrons [L05-005] to e-guns [L05-006] — a controls problem in
the founder's core. Third, interoperability governance: an interface
spec only creates a category if it is genuinely open and technically
disciplined — published early (the red-team repair), versioned, and
proven by a mid-campaign module-swap demonstration.

## 5. Measurable 10x edge

The edge is economic and operational rather than raw physics — and
the P4 score says so honestly (3/5 on ten-x). Against artisanal
chains: catalog lead times (weeks versus quarters), module-swap
service (hours versus site campaigns), and cross-vendor second
sourcing (impossible today by construction). Against the integrated
incumbents: openness itself — an OEM that designs in a ScandiNova
turnkey buys a dependency [L05-047]; one that designs in an open
module family buys a supply base. The benchable metrics: droop and
jitter versus specification, module-swap requalification time, and
cost-per-delivered-joule across the family.

## 6. Demand

Durable demand evidence: Varex's Industrial growth and cargo-order
book [L05-033]; the sterilization-infrastructure base and its
qualification regime [L05-028, L05-042]; the accelerator-lab RF
modernization wave — ESS [L05-007, L05-030], IFMIF-DONES [L05-008],
CEPC's high-efficiency klystron program needing modern drive
[L05-012, L05-013, L05-014], HEPS procurement [L07-045]; DOE's

of the 2024 EtO rule softens the ethylene-oxide-replacement
sub-driver for e-beam sterilization [R10-029] — carried honestly as a
weakened tailwind, not a kill — while ISO 11137-1:2025's raised
energy limits (11 MeV e-beam, 7.5 MeV X-ray) independently ease
e-beam/X-ray adoption [L05-042]. CN-leg evidence: the verifiable
tender stream [L05-036, L07-045] plus the unverified-but-filed CGN
order figures [L05-035].

Bottom-up arithmetic (every input labeled). FACT: the incumbent base
proves volume — 3,000 modulators delivered by one vendor
[P3R2-C-09-S03]. ASSUMPTION: modulator-class drive chains price at
$100-500k per station (modulator-level pricing is unsourced — a
red-team-flagged gap; the 660 kVA/115 kV ESS units [L05-030] anchor
the upper tier). ASSUMPTION: the addressable annual flow across
cargo/NDT recapitalization [L05-033], sterilization capacity, and
lab RF modernization is 100-300 stations/year through the early
2030s. INFERENCE: a $20-100M/yr module-family opportunity at
maturity, entered through single-OEM design-ins rather than market
share assaults. The load-bearing conclusion: one OEM design-in plus
one lab framework agreement funds the platform through launch.

## 7. Competition

ScandiNova is the reference incumbent — 3,000 units, K-series to
100 MW, moving deeper into integration [P3R2-C-09-S03, L05-047].
Jema holds the ESS-class custom-tender franchise [L05-030]. The
former CPI electron-device businesses (now independent, with Palo
Alto/Woodland operations transitioned to MPP Inc. — the corrected
corporate picture [L05-050]) and the tube ecosystem [L05-029] sit
adjacent. In-house OEM teams — including CGN Dasheng's on the CN leg
[L05-035] — are the true volume competitor. Nobody sells an
interoperable cross-OEM module family; the verification pass
confirmed the position is structurally vacant and that the incumbent
is walking away from it, not toward it.

### 7a. Competitive scenarios and response plan

Scenario A (most likely): incumbents keep integrating upward
(turnkey, bundled LLRF [L05-047]) while OEMs keep building in-house;
the open-module position stays vacant into 2028. Response: standard
plan — publish the interface early, win one OEM paid evaluation by
2028 (both red-team repairs), demonstrate the module-swap. Scenario
B: ScandiNova or Jema publishes an open module interface first.
Response: adopt and extend — implement their interface alongside the
venture's own, compete on droop control and service economics; the
category winning matters more than whose document names it, and the
first credible second source still captures the interoperability
premium. Scenario C: thirty years of OEM in-house habit holds — the
red team's strongest counterargument — and no design-in lands by
2033. Response: that is the seed's own kill condition, honored as
written; the fallback before it trips is the retrofit and lab
channels [L05-032, L05-031], where buyers are not OEMs and habit
runs the other way. Scenario D: the CN leg's flagship counterparty
stays counsel-blocked (CGN's Entity-Listed parent [P3R2-C-09-S01]).
Response: the CN chapter proceeds on screened civilian buyers only —
CIRC-class tenders [L05-036] and IHEP-class procurement [L07-045] —
with CGN Dasheng excluded unless counsel clears it; the US leg never
depended on it.

## 8. Decisive 2026-2029 experiment

2027 campaign: build two identical solid-state modules; stack them to
50 kV/500 A pulsed operation; demonstrate 1% droop under digital
compensation across load types; then perform the decisive
interoperability act — swap one module mid-campaign and requalify
against the published acceptance protocol (IEC 60060-1:2025-based
[L05-043]) without bespoke rework. Budget: $500,000 — above the
portfolio band, honestly, priced by HV hardware; staged so the
single-module build gates the stack spend. Thresholds: 1% droop at
50 kV/500 A; jitter within the published spec; module-swap
requalification inside the protocol without redesign. KILL RULE:
failing droop at composition, or a module-swap that requires bespoke
engineering, kills the platform claim and folds the work to custom
modulator engineering. Parallel milestone (red-team repair): the
open interface spec published in 2027, ahead of incumbent
definition; 2028: one OEM paid evaluation signed.

## 9. 2030-2034 timing

No hard window closes — this is a compounding-demand lane, scored
honestly at 3.5 for launch fit. Cargo/NDT recapitalization [L05-033],
sterilization capacity under the eased 2025 qualification frame
[L05-042, R10-029], CN tender cadence [L05-036, L07-045], and lab RF
modernization [L05-007, L05-008, L05-031] all run through 2030 and
beyond. Launch 2030 with an interface spec that has two years of
public life, one OEM evaluation converting to design-in, and the
module family qualified. Adoption 2030-2034: two OEM design-ins, a
lab framework agreement, the retrofit channel [L05-032], and — gated
— screened CN civilian sales. The 2033 no-design-in kill from the
seed remains the standing discipline.

## 11. Side markets

South Korea is substantive: the Vitzro Nextec/PAL klystron

## 12. Regulation, safety, export controls

The platform is dual-use-adjacent: high-energy variants are US-only
by structure, and the CN entity's scope is civilian industrial
modules under the Entity-List discipline above [P3R2-C-09-S01].
Acceptance and safety live on published standards — IEC 60060-1:2025
for HV testing [L05-043], ISO 11137-1:2025 for sterilization
customers' qualification [L05-042] — which is an advantage for a
standards-first vendor. The EtO regulatory direction is uncertain
(repeal proposed [R10-029]) and is treated as upside optionality,
never as a load-bearing driver.

## 13. Capital and business model

v1 capital $6-15M — the heaviest in the top ten after protection
hardware, priced by HV engineering and two qualification campaigns.
Business model: module sales at catalog margins, interface-ecosystem
services (integration, acceptance campaigns), fleet telemetry
subscriptions, and retrofit programs [L05-032]. The margin structure
is deliberately unlike the incumbents': ScandiNova-class vendors earn
on integrated systems and services lock-in [L05-047], while a module
vendor earns on volume across other people's systems — lower price
per sale, structurally larger addressable flow. The $500k experiment
is staged to gate its own spend, and the 2028 OEM evaluation converts
platform credibility into a named customer before launch capital.

## 14. Founder skill transfer

Direct fit: digital droop control, module telemetry, interface
specification, and acceptance instrumentation are founder-native;
kV-to-hundreds-of-kV insulation practice sits at the profile edge —
stated plainly at P4 — and is the first senior hire, mirroring the
packaging-hire discipline of the portfolio's other
hardware-at-the-edge idea (P3R2-A-14). The pattern is deliberate:
where a plan's hardest discipline is not the founder's, the plan
names it, budgets it, and hires it before scaling anything else.

(1) OEM in-house habit holds — the seed's own 2033 kill; scenario C

## 15a. Residual risk assessment

Red-team decision: survive, medium residual risk, medium confidence
— the second of the top ten's two medium-confidence verdicts, and
like the first (P3R2-D-01), the honest character is a real gap
between ecosystem demand and product-specific demand. What disciplines
this plan: the interface-first sequencing creates the category
publicly before capital scales, the OEM evaluation is dated 2028,
and the seed's own 2033 no-design-in kill is preserved unsoftened.

incumbent's response to an open spec — resolve on observable
commercial actions during 2027-2029.
