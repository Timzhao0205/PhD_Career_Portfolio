# Frontier map — where the 24 sit and how they connect

## The organizing frontier

Every selected idea lives on one frontier: **infrastructure classes
scaling faster than their evidence layers.** The 2026-2034 buildouts —
AI datacenters (power and cooling), HTS conductors and magnets,
directed energy, long-distance DC transmission, superhot geothermal,
leading-edge fabs, industrial beams, and electrolysis — are all
procuring hardware ahead of the measurement, qualification, protection,
and control disciplines that mature industries take for granted. The
portfolio sells those disciplines.

## Technical frontier clusters

**1. The AI-datacenter power-and-cooling complex (7 ideas).**
C-01 (800 VDC protection), G-03 (DC acceptance instrumentation), D-19
(kinetic buffering), C-05 (cooling conformance), C-04 (PFAS-free
two-phase loop), F-19 (coolant health), plus E-14's MVDC adjacency.
Shared driver: the NVIDIA/OCP 800 VDC generation (2027) and the
liquid-cooling mandate. Internal logic: C-01 is in-path hardware; G-03
and C-05 are the measurement layers for the same transitions (they
never compete with C-01's socket); C-04/F-19 ride the fluid
transition's qualification and operations phases; D-19 buffers the
load the others qualify. Failure correlation is real but partial —
a slower AI buildout stretches all seven, but the regulatory drivers
(AIM Act, F-gas, GB 40879) run on legislative clocks, not GPU demand.

**2. The HTS/fusion conductor economy (2 ideas + 1 adjacency).**
D-02 (tape acceptance metrology) and D-01 (magnet quench protection)
are upstream and downstream of the same conductor: D-02 qualifies the
tape before winding; D-01 protects the wound magnet in operation. Both
monetize conductor volume rather than fusion success — cables, research
magnets, and naval systems quench and need acceptance regardless.
E-04's cryogenic interconnect shares the cryo-instrumentation skill
base without sharing a market.

**3. Beams and pulsed power (5 ideas).**
D-10 (CBC phase control), C-13 (pump drivers), C-09 (pulsed-power
modules), D-09 (FLASH dosimetry), A-22 (plasma PFAS destruction — a
beam-adjacent plasma system). D-10 and C-13 share the laser-OEM buyer
set (the explicit fold-in path); C-09 drives the tubes and magnetrons
of the beam world; D-09 measures its doses. The defense leg (JLWS,
DE budgets) and the industrial leg (sterilization, cargo scanning,
machining) are independent demand poles for the same electronics
discipline.

**4. The fab process-control layer (2 ideas).**
A-10 (closed-loop ion-energy control) and F-01 (microsecond RF
matching) sit in the same chambers at different layers: F-01 keeps
power delivery matched through pulsed transitions; A-10 regulates what
the delivered power does to the wafer. Same ecosystem, same CHIPS-era
buildout, same Entity-List discipline — and deliberately sequenced
(A-10 first) to avoid competing for the same fab evaluation bandwidth.

**5. Grid and energy conversion (5 ideas).**
E-14 (DC protection/qualification), C-08 (transient-qualified PCHEs),
C-22 (electrolyzer benches), F-23 (envelope controllers), A-14
(300 °C electronics for superhot geothermal). E-14 polices the DC
transmission wave; C-08 and A-14 sell into the thermal frontier
(sCO2, superhot); C-22/F-23 are two non-overlapping layers of the
electrolyzer warranty problem (bench truth vs embedded enforcement).
A-05 (NEG coating) and D-16 (space Brayton) attach here as
big-physics/space infrastructure plays.

**6. CN-primary manufacturing services (1 idea).**
F-16 (plasma surface treatment with inline metrology) stands alone
structurally: the one idea whose deliverable market is China-first,
riding open international tenders in packaging/PCB — the portfolio's
probe of a procurement channel the others cannot use.

## Platform adjacencies (what compounds into what)

- **Referee data platforms:** D-02, C-05, E-14, C-22, G-03, D-09 all
  accumulate cross-vendor datasets whose value compounds with every
  campaign — the KLA/SEL/JEDEC pattern. Any one succeeding
  strengthens the credibility case for the others (shared playbook,
  shared instrument-authority brand).
- **Deterministic control engines:** D-10, A-10, F-01, C-13, F-23
  share the FPGA/EO/closed-loop discipline; IP and talent transfer
  laterally even though markets do not.
- **Harsh-environment qualification:** A-14, C-08, D-16, E-04 share
  the qualify-the-package-first doctrine (thermal cycles, creep,
  cryo) — and the same first-hire profile.
- **Certified safety shelves:** C-01, E-14, D-01 share the
  certification-as-moat economics: one certificate amortized across
  many embeds.

## Market-frontier timing

2026-2027: design-win windows open for D-10 (JBCS), C-01 (Kyber), and
the tender cadence for F-16/C-09. 2027-2028: the portfolio's kill-gate
year — ten Tier-1 verdicts land. 2029-2030: certification completions
(C-01, E-14 relay), commercial triggers (A-14's BH/XGS operation,
D-02's capacity ramp), and launch. 2030-2034: adoption phases per
04_ROADMAP.md, with the referee platforms compounding and the
hardware plays converting design-ins to fleets.

## What the map excludes, deliberately

No consumer products, no software-only plays, no CN-blocked structures
carried as base cases, no India/Singapore market exposure anywhere, and
no idea whose first experiment cannot run by 2028. The frontier is
wide; the portfolio's discipline is that every position on it is
reachable from a bench the founder can build.
