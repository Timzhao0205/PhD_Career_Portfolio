executable by 2028, every one carrying a written kill rule), hold the six
Tier-2 experiments as sequenced follow-ons ($2.08M), and treat the eight
Tier-3 ideas as tracked options ($2.75M) exercised only on their named

leading-edge etch, pulsed power, and electrolysis. No idea requires a
science breakthrough; every one requires disciplined instrumentation,
integration, and certification — the founder's stack.

  deliverable and refuses to fabricate it where it is not.

  ahead of the 2030 launch convention and is handled as pre-company

## What would change this recommendation

A Tier-1 kill-gate failure removes that idea without substitution (the
final 24 and top 10 are frozen identities; any forced change returns to
Stage 40 per the route contract). External events with standing
portfolio effect: an OCP full-methodology release (C-05 scenario B), a
CFS-published protection standard (D-01 scenario B), JBCS awards with no
subsystem carve-out (D-10 scenario B), integrated IEDF-inference
releases (A-10 scenario B), and hydrogen FID collapse (C-22/F-23
scenario D). Each is an observable, dated public event tracked in the
roadmap's monitoring calendar.
