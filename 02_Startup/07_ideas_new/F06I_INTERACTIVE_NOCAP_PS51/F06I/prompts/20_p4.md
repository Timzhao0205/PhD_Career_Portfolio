# 20_p4 — independent scoring and calibration

## Purpose

Score all 65 frozen candidates independently and choose exactly 30 candidates
for adversarial review.

## Inputs allowed

- `src/06/01_MISSION/SCORING_RUBRIC.md`
- `src/06/01_MISSION/SOURCE_STANDARDS.md`
- `src/06/01_MISSION/MISSION_BRIEF.md`
- `src/06/30_SCREENING/LONGLIST.json`
- `src/06/30_SCREENING/EVIDENCE`
- `src/06/10_SOURCE_ATLAS`
- `src/06/90_BIBLIOGRAPHY/sources.json`
- `outputs/10_refresh`

Do not open historical scorecards, selections, deep dives, geography, final
portfolio, audits, or run logs under `src/06`.

## Pilot

Score three candidates from different lanes, test all G1-G7 and all eleven
criteria, verify weighted arithmetic, and compare the three scores globally.
Save only under `pilot/20_p4`.

## Full outputs under `outputs/20_p4`

- `SCORES.json`: `artifact`, `rubric_version`, `calibration_notes`, and exactly
  65 unique `ideas`. Each idea has `idea_id`, `hard_gates`, `criteria`,
  `total_100`, `confidence`, `fatal_uncertainties`, and `disposition`.
- `hard_gates` contains G1-G7, each with Boolean `pass`, `rationale`, and
  `source_ids`.
- `criteria` contains exactly:
  `demonstrated_demand`, `frontier_vision`, `high_end_niche`,
  `competition_whitespace`, `reachable_validation_budget`,
  `technical_elegance`, `ten_x_edge`, `us_china_leverage`,
  `launch_2030_fit`, `expansion_economics`, and `founder_skill_transfer`.
  Each criterion has `score_0_5`, `weighted_points`, `rationale`, and
  `source_ids`.
- `SURVIVORS.json`: `artifact`, `selection_rule`, and exactly 30 unique
  `ideas`; each has `idea_id`, `p4_rank`, `total_100`, `gate_status`, and
  `p5_focus`.
- `P4_REPORT.md`: calibration, distributions, gate failures, lane comparison,
  and cut line.
- `RESULT.json`: `stage:"20_p4"`, `status:"COMPLETE"`, `outputs`, and checks.

Scores above 3 require cited evidence. Apply the rubric weights literally and
reconcile rounding. Penalize evidence gaps rather than filling them with
intuition. The main Fable agent owns the final scores and survivor set.
