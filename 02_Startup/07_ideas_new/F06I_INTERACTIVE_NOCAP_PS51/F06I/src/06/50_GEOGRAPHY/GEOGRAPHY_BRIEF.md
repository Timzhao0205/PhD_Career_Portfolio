# Geography brief: United States and China

**Evidence cutoff:** 14 July 2026  
**Portfolio:** frozen P5 final 24  
**Use:** precompany validation from 2026-2029, a conditional 2030 launch, and trigger monitoring through 2034

## Decision frame

The portfolio contains **21 United States beachheads, 18 China beachheads, and 15 dual-market ideas**. The asymmetry is intentional. Six ideas are United States only: `P3R2-D-01`, `P5-USSCI2-S01`, `P3R2-E-14`, `P3R2-A-14`, `P3R2-E-04`, and `P3R2-F-06`. Three are China only: `P3R2-B-01`, `P3R2-F-12`, and `P3R2-G-01`. Every other idea has a separate route in both primary markets; “dual” does not mean one product, entity, data room, or qualification dossier can automatically serve both.

This is a route-to-validation brief, not a forecast of revenue or a claim that access has been secured. No signed pilot, purchase order, tender eligibility, laboratory access, or data right is assumed. Cross-border control and ownership statements are operating hypotheses that require refreshed specialist review before transactions; they are not legal conclusions.

The demand anchors are institutional rather than top-down market estimates. In the United States these include federal laboratories and competitive solicitations, equipment OEMs, power-project developers, industrial operators, hyperscalers, commissioning firms, and regulated infrastructure owners. In China they include ministry programs, public research institutes, industrial OEMs, eligible tendering fabricators, classification bodies, commercial datacenter operators, shipyards, and project integrators. Examples in the accepted ledger include the [U.S. fusion-program selections](https://www.energy.gov/articles/us-department-energy-announces-selectees-107-million-fusion-innovation-research-engine), a [Brookhaven hybrid MCP-PMT request for information](https://sam.gov/opp/c4efe4e23fad4659885b0591235e2714/view), [advanced-packaging X-ray procurement in Guangzhou](https://chinabidding.mofcom.gov.cn/bidDetail/bidding/bulletin/202602/ff8080819a82040b019c98b9dc8a140c.html), and a [HEPS vacuum-chamber tender](https://www.ccnta.cn/article/7832.html).

## United States operating model

The United States route is strongest where a company can enter through an open benchmark, paid evaluation, federal R&D subcontract, or OEM qualification before it must carry full system liability. Scientific hardware should begin with a laboratory or tube/magnet/quantum-system integrator and a narrow interface-controlled deliverable. The BNL dynode RFI, [NIST X-ray system acquisition](https://sam.gov/workspace/contract/opp/96effae327354fae8e9363b8b2a98541/view), [DOE quantum-center continuation](https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-announces-625-million-advance-next-phase-national-quantum-information), and [FORGE geothermal R&D route](https://www.energy.gov/hgeo/geothermal/forge-rd) show distinct procurement paths; they do not prove that a startup will be selected.

For datacenter and industrial power hardware, the first buyer is more likely an OEM, rack/power-sidecar designer, cooling integrator, or commissioning specialist than the hyperscaler itself. Qualification must therefore be packaged as interface specifications, traceable test data, failure-mode evidence, and an installable reference design. The [Project Deschutes CDU specification](https://arpa-e.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2026-01/Day1_06b_Google_Kabbani.pdf) is useful as a buyer-generated performance boundary, while [IEC 62477-1](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/28936) is a baseline safety reference for converter equipment. Product-specific certification plans still need confirmation with the actual integrator and test laboratory.

For grid and hydrogen ideas, project timing and bankability matter more than an impressive isolated prototype. The route runs through developer/OEM test programs, hardware-in-the-loop evidence, protection studies, and warranty-recognized data. The [Southern Spirit HVDC project](https://patternenergy.com/projects/southern-spirit-transmission/) provides a dated project anchor, and the [hydrogen-hub award record](https://www.energy.gov/articles/biden-harris-administration-announces-awards-22-billion-two-regional-clean-hydrogen-hubs) identifies prospective asset networks. Neither source guarantees a merchant sensing, controller, relay, or degradation-bench socket.

## China operating model

China is not a generic direct-sales extension. The credible routes are: sell to a qualified local OEM; license or jointly develop with a thermal, laser, semiconductor-equipment, ship-power, or vacuum-system integrator; or sell calibrated equipment and recurring verification to a China-registered fabricator that can bid. Direct pursuit of sensitive public infrastructure and direct hyperscaler selling are false routes where the P5 record says access is structurally weak.

Qualification is institution-specific. Datacenter thermal and power products need local system integration and evidence aligned with the evolving [GB 40879 datacenter-efficiency standard](https://std.samr.gov.cn/gb/search/gbDetailed?id=2E4DD4D8E2E84A4BE06397BE0A0AE354), [T/CIEP 0263-2025 liquid-cooling specification](https://www.ndls.org.cn/standard/detail/f4af7d31f9d7cfe71b0a742752250077), and the official [AI-energy action plan](https://www.nea.gov.cn/20260508/4dae97ca01d348e4871bb8654be34b3a/c.html). Marine power must enter through shipyard/integrator design and China Classification Society review; the accepted evidence includes the [2025 battery-powered-ship rules](https://www.ccs.org.cn/ccswz/articleDetail?id=202601060114611630&columnId=201900002000000398) and [pure-battery inspection rules](https://www.ccs.org.cn/ccswz/articleDetail?id=202503061077276480&columnId=202007070926126287). Industrial heat-pump metrology should align with the [national heat-pump action plan](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202504/P020250402477391648460.pdf) and [2026-2028 actual-load-efficiency program](https://www.miit.gov.cn/xwfb/xwfbh/bxwfbh/art/2026/art_4e9257192fe743ca84c33ffc78e94e0c.html).

Local data control is also a commercial issue: fab recipes, laser waveforms, datacenter power sequences, warranty logs, and facility acceptance records may remain on the customer site. The precompany product should be designed for on-premise analysis, exportable summary metrics, separated model-update packages, and customer-controlled raw data. The China moat cannot rest on a transferable recipe alone; calibration traceability, reference artifacts, sequence libraries, field service, and continuously updated qualification dossiers are safer starting points.

## 2026-2034 engagement and launch sequence

**Second half 2026:** build institution maps by idea, identify the actual qualification owner, and request only non-confidential interface and acceptance information. Track open solicitations, public test plans, standards revisions, and named commissioning providers. For each dual-market idea, maintain separate U.S. and China buyer hypotheses, bills of material, raw-data rules, and claims matrices.

**2027:** run the bounded technical tests already frozen in P5. The low-cost commercial-gate ideas should lead with RFQs, paid evaluations, make-versus-buy interviews, or dossier requirements. Scientific and industrial hardware should use university, national-lab, OEM, rental-equipment, or integrator benches only after written test scope and data ownership are established. A test result is not procurement engagement unless a buyer or integrator participates in a defined evaluation.

**2028:** convert surviving prototypes into qualification artifacts: calibration chains, environmental and lifetime data, fault trees, interface-control documents, service procedures, and customer-specific acceptance templates. In China, verify local tender eligibility and decide whether the route is equipment sale, license, joint development, or recurring calibration. In the United States, identify whether the buyer is a federal prime/subcontract route, an OEM design-in, a project developer, or an independent commissioning firm.

**2029 hard gates:** eleven ideas already have a credible procurement-engagement path in P5, but none may be treated as secured. Require documentary evidence appropriate to the idea: paid evaluation, funded qualification task, buyer-approved interface, class boundary, frame-agreement pathway, or named project inclusion. Re-run incumbent, schedule, standards, ownership, export-control, and component-availability checks. Kill or narrow any false merchant socket rather than carrying it into the 2030 portfolio.

**2030 launch:** launch only the surviving narrow product and only in the market where qualification is complete. A dual-market label does not require simultaneous launch. Serviceable early configurations are calibration equipment, test benches, retrofit kits, qualified modules, and documented subsystems—not full plants, fabs, vessels, grids, or datacenters.

**2030-2034:** monitor the triggers recorded in the matrix: serial HTS magnet builds; post-freeze detector funding; multi-terminal DC projects; advanced-packaging yield learning; industrial heat-pump acceptance; qualified non-PFAS fluids; marine class boundaries; recurring chamber tenders; paid third-party DC commissioning; financed hydrogen plants; and merchant sCO2/geothermal OEM sockets. A slip is not automatically failure, but repeated absence of the named buyer action is.

## Refresh-sensitive risks

1. **Public-program and project dates:** re-check fusion, detector, HVDC, geothermal, heat-pump, hydrogen, CEPC/HEPS, advanced-packaging, and sCO2 schedules at least quarterly from 2028.
2. **Standards and acceptance ownership:** refresh IEC, GB, liquid-cooling, ship-class, environmental-fluid, and grid-protection requirements before design freeze; determine which document is mandatory, contractual, or merely informative for the named buyer.
3. **Incumbent integration:** repeat make-versus-buy work for plasma matching, laser drivers, PCHEs, cooling, DC protection, wideband sensing, and turbo-generator cartridges. The merchant socket can disappear faster than the physics changes.
4. **Data and site access:** obtain written scope for fab recipes, detector interfaces, magnet faults, heat balances, warranty logs, and commissioning records. Until then, all proposed partner tests remain uncommitted.
5. **Cross-border separation:** re-screen ownership, end use, components, technical-data movement, and customer restrictions before every China/U.S. transfer. Commercial laser, semiconductor, grid, and ship-power lines need especially conservative separation.
6. **False beachheads:** do not silently convert adjacent activity into market entry. U.S. activity does not validate the three China-only ideas; China projects do not validate the six U.S.-only ideas; state-grid awards, base-ePIC procurement, direct Chinese hyperscaler sales, and ineligible direct big-science tendering remain explicitly excluded routes.
