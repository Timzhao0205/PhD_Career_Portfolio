# Source and evidence policy

- Search snippets are discovery aids, not accepted evidence. Open the source.
- Prefer primary buyer/procurement records, filings, standards organizations,
  regulators, government data, and peer-reviewed research.
- Record claim-to-source traceability and distinguish fact, inference, and
  hypothesis.
- Current mutable claims should prefer 2025-2026 evidence; retain older sources
  for stable technical facts and historical baselines.
- US and China are the primary markets. Japan, Taiwan, and South Korea are
  optional side markets. India and Singapore are excluded as markets.
- Entirely India-origin sources are ineligible. Multinational academic work is
  eligible only when a non-Indian institutional affiliation is affirmatively
  verified.
- The intended company launch is 2030. Analyze preparation during 2026-2029 and
  demand windows during 2030-2034.
- Do not invent source IDs, bibliographic metadata, dates, access status, buyer
  behavior, or quantitative performance.
- Material uncertainty and unavailable evidence must remain explicit.
