## 2. The shared-stack logic

B20's full-universe finding is the design constraint: across 39 directions,
every non-WEAK alignment runs through exactly one mechanism family —
**measurement authority** — via two channels: (a) the *demonstrated*
hardware channel (Hall readout chains C03/C13, harsh-environment packaging
C46/C01, EMI-disciplined bench measurement), and (b) the *proposed*
traceable-calibration + estimator-honesty methodology channel (C06 WP-C;
C23/C31 FT-02). Nothing else in the PhD transfers anywhere in the universe,
and the PhD's most distinctive scientific claims (stellarator deployment,
GaN radiation question, hybrid coil→Hall direction) carry the least startup
value (B20 §8.3).

A capability belongs in the shared stack only if it simultaneously:

1. **serves a PhD outcome** — Opt2 Element 1 (calibration credential, also
   the G5 gate condition), Element 2 (hybrid/bandwidth work), Element 3 (T0
   estimator package), or the P1/P2/P3 publication path (C38); and
2. **serves multiple startup directions, not one** — the option-preserving
   preference, made explicit: W1's measurement-and-qualification family
   (G-03, C-05, D-09, F-06) and/or W2's magnet-power measurement/detection
   family (D-01, ST01-C10/C11, ST03-ID_08/12), rather than any single
   idea's bespoke stack; and
3. **keeps fallbacks alive** — the OPT3 reconstruction fallback (C47) and
   the far-domain method market the CN-01 record evidences.

Capabilities that fail test 2 (idea-specific stacks: converter design,
protection interruption, certification, electrochemistry, photonics,
manufacturing, field trades) are deliberately routed to
`collaborator_or_vendor` or excluded — B25 §4's conclusion carried forward,
not re-litigated. Two disclosed exceptions to test 2 are kept anyway with
their scope stated: S8 (lock-in thermography — D-02-specific, kept because
the record names it for the primary alignment candidate and correction 1
requires saying it is not possessed) and BR-I (BT-7 — retained unscheduled
so no ladder ID is orphaned).

**The stack's honest center of gravity** (B25 §5, unchanged): the founder's
realistic near-term capability growth is entirely on the
measurement/qualification side — close C04 → execute WP-C (C06, = BR-D) →
pass FT-02 (C31, = BR-A Phase 0) → exercise the methodology on current
measurands (BR-B). Both wedges are bets on that conversion. The bridge
ladder is exactly that conversion, instrumented with stop/continue gates.
