# B50 — execution, IP/collaboration, and manual work

Translate the combined portfolio into a realistic 2026–2034 roadmap. Separate
research milestones, evidence gates, skill development, customer discovery,
prototype/qualification work, IP screening, regulation/certification, funding,
and launch choices. Use B15 gaps and boundary conditions to define experiments;
do not convert literature suggestions into demonstrated readiness. Do not treat
patent screening as legal advice.

Identify tasks that require the user, advisor, laboratory, licensed engineer,
test house, lawyer, customer, supplier, or regulator. Do not let an AI-generated
report impersonate those approvals. Include branch points if Opt2, a power
bridge, or a selected startup direction fails.

Required outputs:

- `ROADMAP.md`: dated milestones, owners, dependencies, artifacts, success/kill
  gates, and fallback branches through 2034.
- `IP_COLLAB.md`: IP/FTO questions, collaboration map, data/publication
  boundaries, and questions for qualified professionals.
- `MANUAL_WORK.md`: precise human actions, preparation, evidence to bring, and
  decisions that cannot be automated.
- `SOURCES.csv`: current primary sources for standards/regulatory claims.

Pilot: a 90-day slice for one sensing bridge and one power bridge.
