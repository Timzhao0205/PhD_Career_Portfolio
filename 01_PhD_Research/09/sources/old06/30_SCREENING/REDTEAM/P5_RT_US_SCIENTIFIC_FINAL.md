# P5 final US scientific red team

The final comparison challenged three concepts against literal G1-G7, with special attention to in-house laboratory systems and whether any procurement socket remains open for a company launched in 2030.

## Selected: `P5-USSCI2-S01`

The diamond transmission-dynode cartridge survives at a revised 58.2/100 and medium-low confidence. Its unusually strong fact is BNL RFI 479547: the buyer explicitly seeks outside organizations to procure, process, thin, apply NEA treatment to, characterize, and help integrate diamond film for the named hybrid MCP-PMT LDRD. That is the exact necessary supplier job, not generic detector spending.

The bear case remains material. Incom, Exosens/Photonis, Hamamatsu, and BNL already control complete MCP-PMT or detector architectures. The startup cannot sell “a better photon detector.” Its only non-cosmetic boundary is a qualified upstream diamond membrane/process cartridge that adds transmission gain while reducing charge demanded from the MCP. The proposed nine-month experiment must show repeatable yield, bake and dose life, low leakage, acceptable timing spread, and a sealable interface. Failure on any of those points kills the company.

The timing thesis was narrowed. Base ePIC photosensor work freezes before 2030 and is not counted. The 2030 route exists only if EIC Detector II, upgrades/spares, or another US fast-photon program funds external dynode hardware. Official EIC work extends through 2035, but the future package is not committed. End-2029 paid qualification or funded design-in is therefore a hard commercial kill.

Gate result: G1 PASS; G2 PASS; G3 PASS_MARGINAL; G4 PASS_MARGINAL; G5 PASS; G6 PASS; G7 PASS_MARGINAL. The 15-source pack contains seven peer-reviewed records and six records normalized to the canonical primary-demand types.

## Killed: `P5-USSCI2-S02`

The liquid-metal flowmeter is killed for selection. PPPL's rotating Lorentz-force flowmeter work and newly initiated ultrasonic diagnostic are direct in-house substitutes. The proposed common-mode reference is technically interesting, but it has not yet shown the required field rejection or a merchant boundary the laboratory cannot absorb. A sub-$100k bench is not credited because the failed competition gate controls selection.

## Killed on timing: `P5-USSCI3-S01`

The EIC polarimetry requirement is exact and scientifically important, but the detailed schedule awards and receives detector/electronics work in 2027-2028. Without an independent funded post-freeze upgrade or replacement trigger, a 2030 launch is too late. The concept is therefore not counted despite the quality of the technical need.
