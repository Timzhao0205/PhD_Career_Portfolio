# D10 — P3R2-C-22: Electrolyzer degradation-emulation and bankability test systems

## 1. Thesis

The hydrogen industry writes twenty-year stack warranties on
degradation science it does not yet possess, and the test-equipment
layer that could close that gap does not exist as a product. FACT: the
peer-reviewed literature now documents exactly how electrolyzers die
under real operating conditions — anode-catalyst aging under
intermittent operation [L11-001], membrane and catalyst degradation
pathways [L11-002], decomposed voltage-decay budgets across membrane,
catalyst layer, and porous transport [L11-003], converter current
ripple measurably accelerating degradation [L11-021], hydrogen
crossover-induced failure [L11-031], and a 2026 review synthesizing
intermittency-specific mechanisms [L11-004] — while industrial solid-
oxide systems need ≤0.5-0.75%/kh degradation to hold five-year
performance [L11-014, L11-015]. FACT: the field consequences are
public — Sinopec's flagship Kuqa project ran at ~20% of rated capacity
in 2023, with electrolyzer inefficiency at reduced loads named among
the causes [L11-049]. FACT: no settled qualification standard exists
— the EU's own science service published its first proposed
harmonized accelerated-stress-test protocols only in June 2024
[P3R2-C-22-S01] — and the incumbent test-equipment vendor, Greenlight
Innovation, sells constant-current/constant-voltage stations up to
4 MW with no programmable ripple synthesis or renewable-profile
playback anywhere in its line [P3R2-C-22-S02] (re-verified 2026-07).
INFERENCE: warranties, project finance, and tender acceptance all
need degradation truth that neither OEM marketing nor generic benches
provide. HYPOTHESIS: programmable degradation-emulation benches —
synthesizing converter ripple, renewable intermittency, and start-stop
stress with per-mechanism diagnostic attribution — become the
bankability instrument of the electrolyzer era, selling degradation
truth to lenders, buyers, and tender authorities on both sides of the
Pacific.

## 2. Customer job and named buyers

The customer job is to convert a warranty clause or a tender
specification into measured engineering: play back twenty years of
projected electrical stress in ninety days, attribute the observed
decay to mechanisms, and hand the result to a counterparty as
evidence. Named buyers, US leg: the surviving Regional Clean Hydrogen
Hub cohort — five of seven hubs preserved as of April 2026 with
almost $5B of combined federal cost share (HyVelocity up to $1.2B,
MachH2 up to $1B, ARCH2, MACH2, Heartland) [P3R2-C-22-S03, L11-032]
— whose project sponsors and lenders must underwrite stack lifetime;
and the OEMs whose economics now turn on warranty exposure — Plug
Power with $187M of 2025 electrolyzer revenue and 300+ MW shipped
[L11-046], Nel with a NOK 1.3B backlog and its largest-ever PEM order
[L11-047], thyssenkrupp nucera carrying a green-hydrogen segment at
negative EBIT while chlor-alkali profits [L11-048]. Named buyers, CN
leg: the tender-acceptance world — China Energy Construction's
14-supplier qualification framework and the Songyuan 64-set award
across six vendors [L11-051]; the exporting vendor cohort (LONGi,
Sungrow Hydrogen, CSSC Peric, Guofu) whose overseas contracts create
third-party acceptance demand [L11-050]; all under the reaffirmed
national 2030 target of 2 Mt/yr renewable hydrogen in the 15th
Five-Year energy plan [P3R2-C-22-S04]. Reference-installation owners
(Siemens Energy's 100 MW Hamburg and 54 MW BASF units [L11-040],
Accelera's HyLYZER class [L11-041]) mark the fleet whose second
generation buys qualification.

## 3. Product boundary and architecture

The product is a bench family plus a data product: (a) programmable
stress-synthesis power electronics — arbitrary ripple spectra
[L11-021], renewable-profile playback, start-stop and open-circuit
protocols [L11-001] — at single-cell to short-stack scale; (b) a
diagnostic layer running impedance spectroscopy and voltage-decay
decomposition in the frame the literature established [L11-003];
(c) accelerated-protocol libraries aligned to the EU JRC proposals
[P3R2-C-22-S01] so results map onto the emerging harmonization
rather than competing with it; and (d) the cross-vendor degradation
dataset — the compounding asset, in the same family as this
portfolio's other method-authority plays. Explicitly outside the
boundary: electrolyzers themselves, gigawatt factory acceptance
lines, and generic constant-current stations — the Greenlight
hardware layer is conceded [P3R2-C-22-S02]; the wedge is stress
synthesis plus attribution. Adjacent instrument classes (nanohm
weld-resistance meters and formation-line equipment in the battery
world [L11-043], ultra-high-precision coulometry [L11-044]) are the
design references for what measurement-grade discipline looks like
when an electrochemical industry matures.

## 4. Feasibility

The bench is established test-and-measurement engineering; the
science risk is attribution fidelity, and it is framed honestly.
Three sub-problems. First, stress synthesis: generating programmable
ripple spectra and profile playback at kilowatt-to-50 kW scale is
power-electronics work squarely in the founder's stack; the
literature already quantifies which spectral features matter
[L11-021]. Second, attribution: decomposing observed decay into
mechanism contributions requires the impedance and post-mortem frame
of the degradation literature [L11-003, L11-002, L11-031] and an
electrochemistry collaborator — the outside dependency named at P4
— because instrument authority without interpretive authority is a
data logger. Third, acceleration validity: the premise that 90-day
accelerated campaigns predict field life is the industry's open
question, not the venture's private one — the EU protocols are
"proposed," not settled [P3R2-C-22-S01], SOEC modeling shows
operating-mode-dependent lifetimes [L11-015, L11-014] — so the
decisive experiment tests reproduction of known literature
signatures first, and the dataset strategy (accumulating
bench-versus-field pairs) is the long answer.

## 5. Measurable 10x edge

Against the incumbent bench: stress classes it cannot generate at
all — no ripple synthesis, no profile playback [P3R2-C-22-S02] —
which are precisely the stresses the literature says drive real
degradation [L11-021, L11-004, L11-001]. Against OEM in-house labs:
neutrality — an OEM's own lifetime claims are exactly what lenders
discount; the third-party dataset is unpurchasable internally.
The quantitative edge: twenty years of projected electrical stress
compressed into a 90-day campaign with per-mechanism attribution —
if the science holds, an order-of-magnitude compression of the
qualification timeline that currently gates FIDs.

## 6. Demand

Durable demand evidence: the surviving hub cohort's ~$5B cost share
[P3R2-C-22-S03, L11-032]; OEM warranty economics across three public
financial profiles [L11-046, L11-047, L11-048]; the CN tender and
export cadence [L11-051, L11-050] under a dated national target
[P3R2-C-22-S04]; and field underperformance already costing real
projects real output [L11-049]. Refresh-sensitive evidence: the US
envelope was cut — ARCHES and PNWH2 rescinded in October 2025
[R10-025] — and the honest demand case uses the five-hub survivor
list, not the original seven. The anti-fragile framing verified at
P4 holds on either industry branch: boom drives acceptance testing
at tender scale (CN), consolidation drives warranty credibility
(US/EU) — both branches buy benches.

A second demand layer deserves separate statement: dispute
resolution. When a delivered fleet underperforms — the Kuqa pattern
[L11-049] — the parties need an instrument-grade answer to whose
fault it is: power supply, operating profile, or stack. Nobody
neutral can supply that answer today, and the first vendor whose
bench and protocols are cited in a warranty settlement acquires the
same referee authority this portfolio's metrology ideas pursue in
their own lanes.

Bottom-up arithmetic (every input labeled). ASSUMPTION: a
stress-synthesis bench with diagnostics sells for $300k-1M and a
paid qualification campaign for $100-400k — bench-campaign pricing
is unsourced, a red-team-flagged gap, anchored to the 4 MW-class
generic-station market's existence [P3R2-C-22-S02]. ASSUMPTION: the
buyer universe by 2030 is 30-60 organizations (OEMs on three
continents [L11-046, L11-047, L11-048, L11-050], hub sponsors and
lenders [P3R2-C-22-S03], CN tender authorities and their qualified
vendor pools [L11-051]). INFERENCE: a $15-60M cumulative
bench-and-campaign opportunity through 2034, with the degradation
dataset as the premium tier. The load-bearing conclusion: two bench
sales plus a handful of campaigns fund the company; the thesis needs
the hydrogen industry to keep existing, not to boom.

## 7. Competition

Greenlight Innovation owns the generic hardware layer — 4 MW/30 kA
stations, decades of installed base — and is the vendor most able
to add ripple synthesis if the category proves out
[P3R2-C-22-S02]. Chroma/Hioki-class T&M vendors could enter
sideways from the battery world [L11-043, L11-044]. OEM in-house
labs are the volume incumbent. The EU JRC harmonization process
could — in the best failure mode — publish free reference
protocols with reference hardware [P3R2-C-22-S01]. Nobody today
sells stress synthesis plus attribution plus a financier-facing
dataset; the 2026-07 verification confirmed the wedge is open.

### 7a. Competitive scenarios and response plan

Scenario A (most likely): Greenlight keeps selling generic
stations; harmonization crawls; OEM labs stay internal. Response:
standard plan — bench, campaign, dataset, with the EU-protocol
alignment making the venture the practical implementation of the
emerging standard [P3R2-C-22-S01]. Scenario B: Greenlight adds
ripple synthesis. Response: the dataset and attribution layer are
the moat — hardware parity without the cross-vendor degradation
library and lender relationships leaves the authority seat
unclaimed; partner-or-compete optionality (their installed base,
this venture's protocols) stays open. Scenario C: JRC publishes a
complete harmonized method with free reference implementations.
Response: same join-don't-race posture as the portfolio's other
metrology plays — implement the standard first, sell certified
campaigns under it; standards create markets for their best
implementers. Scenario D: hydrogen FIDs collapse on both legs — the
red team's strongest counterargument. Response: the cost base is a
bench and a small team; the CN acceptance-testing branch [L11-051]
and OEM warranty-defense work in consolidation both persist in a
shrinking industry; the seed's own kill (no sale by end-2033)
remains the honest exit line.

## 8. Decisive 2026-2029 experiment

2027 campaign: build a 50 kW programmable-stress bench (ripple
synthesis across the literature's spectral range [L11-021],
renewable-profile playback, start-stop protocols [L11-001]); run a
90-day accelerated campaign on two commercial short stacks from
different vendors; attempt per-mechanism attribution of observed
decay [L11-003]. Budget: $250,000. Thresholds: reproduce at least
two literature-documented degradation signatures on command
(ripple-driven resistance growth [L11-021]; intermittency-driven
catalyst decay [L11-001]); attribution decomposition consistent
with impedance and post-campaign diagnostics; protocol library
mapped onto the JRC frame [P3R2-C-22-S01]. KILL RULE: if commanded
stress cannot reproduce known signatures, or attribution cannot
distinguish mechanisms, the data-product premise dies and the work
folds to custom test-stand engineering. Follow-on (red-team dated
repair): one lender, hub developer, or tender authority converted
into a paid pilot campaign by 2028; EU harmonization status
refreshed annually.

## 9. 2030-2034 timing

The window persists on either industry branch, which is the timing
thesis's strength and its ceiling: no hard trigger forces adoption,
but every year of fleet growth [L11-046, L11-040] and every
warranty dispute adds pressure. Launch 2030 with three years of
protocol history and the first bench-versus-field data pairs.
Adoption 2030-2034: benches in two OEM labs and one CN acceptance
channel; campaign revenue from hub-cohort projects as they reach
FID [P3R2-C-22-S03]; the dataset tier priced for lenders. If the
2020s end with hydrogen smaller than promised, the venture is a
profitable niche instrument maker; if the 2 Mt CN target
[P3R2-C-22-S04] and hub buildout hold, it is the qualification
authority of a real industry.

## 10. Independent US and China paths

US path: hub-sponsor and lender campaigns [P3R2-C-22-S03,
L11-032], OEM warranty-defense work [L11-046], and eventual
standard-implementation services as harmonization matures
[P3R2-C-22-S01]. China path (independently evidenced): tender
acceptance testing inside the CEEC-class procurement machinery
[L11-051], vendor export-qualification campaigns [L11-050], and
the policy tailwind of the dated national target [P3R2-C-22-S04].
Export posture: test equipment of this class is uncontrolled; the
named CN suppliers screen clean on available checks (stage-20/30
verification); entry follows the standard instrument-sales
playbook. The two legs buy for different reasons — US buys
bankability, CN buys acceptance — and the same bench serves both,
which is the healthiest kind of dual-market structure in this
portfolio.

## 11. Side markets

The platform extends by electrochemistry, not geography: flow
batteries, CO2 electrolysis, and fuel-cell qualification share the
stress-synthesis and attribution core, and the battery industry's
instrument maturity [L11-043, L11-044] shows where the category
ends up — nanohm-resolution weld inspection and microamp-precision
coulometry became purchasable exactly when that industry's warranty
stakes demanded them, a two-decade preview of the electrolyzer
instrument market's trajectory. Japan/Korea enter through the same
OEM channels as Europe; none is load-bearing.

## 12. Regulation, safety, export controls

No settled certification regime exists — the JRC proposals are the
first EU-level harmonization step [P3R2-C-22-S01], and alignment
with them is product strategy, not compliance burden. Hydrogen lab
safety (crossover monitoring [L11-031], ventilation, pressure) is
established practice and is engineered into the bench itself, since
induced crossover is one of the failure modes the campaigns
deliberately provoke. Export: uncontrolled instrument class; no
trigger identified on either leg.

## 13. Capital and business model

v1 capital $4-10M: bench productization, two campaign teams, and
the dataset platform. Business model: bench sales, paid
qualification campaigns (the near-term revenue and the dataset
feedstock), protocol licensing as harmonization matures, and the
lender-facing data tier as the compounding asset. The $250k
experiment — the portfolio's second-cheapest — buys the entire
attribution question before any of that capital is committed.

## 15. Failure modes

(1) Hydrogen FID collapse on both legs — scenario D; the seed's
2033 no-sale kill stands. (2) Greenlight adds stress synthesis —
scenario B's dataset moat. (3) OEM labs keep qualification
internal — countered by lender-side and tender-side entry, the
buyers OEMs cannot satisfy with self-testing. (4) Free harmonized
reference implementations — scenario C's implementer posture.
(5) Attribution science fails — caught at $250k by the kill rule,
and the fold-back (custom test stands) is a real, smaller
business.

## 15a. Residual risk assessment

Red-team decision: survive, medium residual risk, high confidence.
The strongest counterargument — test equipment scales with its
industry's capex, and hydrogen's order books are shrinking — is
carried openly: this is the top ten's clearest bet on an industry
rather than on physics, and it is priced accordingly (cheapest
experiment but one, modest v1, profitable-niche downside). The
dominant uncertainties — FID trajectory and attribution science —
resolve respectively on public order books through 2027-2028 and
on the venture's own $250k campaign. The idea earns its deep-dive
slot as the portfolio's electrochemical diversification: its
demand drivers are uncorrelated with the datacenter, defense, and
fusion legs that carry the rest of the top ten.
