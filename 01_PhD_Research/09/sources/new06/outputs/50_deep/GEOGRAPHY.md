export-control boundaries for every idea in `outputs/40_select/SELECTION.json`,
in final rank order. "Independent" means each leg stands on its own named
evidence; no leg is inferred from the other. Where a leg is absent, the
absence is a verified adjudication, not an omission. Portfolio counts:
23 US beachheads, 14 China beachheads, 13 dual-market structures, 1
CN-primary structure (P3R2-F-16), 10 US-only structures.

## Per-idea geography

### 1. P3R2-D-02 — Reel-to-reel contactless REBCO tape metrology (dual)

US path: CFS-ecosystem magnet builders and national labs (US ITER/ORNL
channel), NIST-traceable co-calibration, DOE Milestone cohort as named
prospects. China path (independent): Shanghai Superconductor's contracted
HL-4 volume and 20,000 km/yr capacity target create producer-side
acceptance demand in the vendor's own disclosures, inside Shanghai's
eighteen-year HTS industry chain; State Grid's operating cable and
ASIPP/CFETR magnet programs are buyer-side poles. Side markets: Japan
(Furukawa/SuperPower supply chain), South Korea (KEPCO/LS cable lineage).
Export boundary: the instrument class carries no identified control
trigger; the operative gate is conclusive BIS Consolidated Screening List
resolution for WST and ASIPP before any CN transaction (inconclusive
negatives to date).

## Cross-portfolio observations

1. **Independence discipline.** Every dual-market idea carries separately
   evidenced legs (buyer-authored documents on each side); no CN leg is a
   mirror of a US case. Where original CN structures failed compliance
   (A-10, F-01, C-13), the record shows the repair, the gate, and the
   base-case exclusion rather than a quiet keep.
2. **Compliance gates are dated and named.** Six ideas carry explicit
   counterparty or classification gates (D-02 screening, E-14 EAR review,
   A-10 2027 counsel gate, C-09 CGN sign-off, C-13 partition, F-01
   AMEC-direct terms). Each is a go/no-go with an owner and a date, not a
   standing caveat.
3. **Side markets are optional everywhere.** Japan, Korea, and Taiwan
   appear only as additive channels; no plan's economics require them —
   consistent with the route's market policy (US/CN primary; JP/TW/KR
   optional; India and Singapore excluded as markets throughout).
4. **The US-only cluster is deliberate.** Ranks 4, 6, 7, 11, 16, 17, 18,
   20, 24 (plus E-14's US-primary structure) are US-only for reasons of
   law (ITAR, domestic-firm restrictions), verified CN absence, or
   IP-protection adjudications — the portfolio buys its US/China balance
   from ideas where dual delivery is real, not from forcing blocked legs.
