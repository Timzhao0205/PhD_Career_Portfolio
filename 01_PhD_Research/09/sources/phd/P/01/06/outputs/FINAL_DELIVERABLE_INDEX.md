## 2. Recommended reading order

**For Tim (decision-first):**

1. [`FINAL_EXECUTIVE_STRATEGY.md`](FINAL_EXECUTIVE_STRATEGY.md) — the
   whole answer in one document.
2. [`FINAL_ACTION_PLAN.md`](FINAL_ACTION_PLAN.md) — what to do, when, and
   what not to do yet.
3. [`06_ADVISOR_MEETING_BRIEF.md`](06_ADVISOR_MEETING_BRIEF.md) — take to
   the meeting (with the §8 materials list of the action plan).
4. [`02_RESEARCH_DIRECTION_DECISION.md`](02_RESEARCH_DIRECTION_DECISION.md)
   — the full reasoning behind the verdict, when questioned on any point.
5. [`03_PUBLICATION_ROUTE_DECISION.md`](03_PUBLICATION_ROUTE_DECISION.md)
   + [`03_MANUSCRIPT_DIAGNOSIS.md`](03_MANUSCRIPT_DIAGNOSIS.md) — before
   touching the manuscript.
6. [`04_HSX_EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md`](04_HSX_EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md) (+ its two
   companion plans and the requirements CSV) — before bench work starts.
7. [`05_DISCLOSURE_HOLD_CHECKLIST.md`](05_DISCLOSURE_HOLD_CHECKLIST.md) +
   [`05_CANDIDATE_PROTECTABLE_CONCEPTS.md`](05_CANDIDATE_PROTECTABLE_CONCEPTS.md)
   — before any disclosure event or the OTL conversation.
8. [`06_24_MONTH_PHD_ROADMAP.md`](06_24_MONTH_PHD_ROADMAP.md) +
   [`06_MILESTONES.csv`](06_MILESTONES.csv) — the operating calendar.
9. [`07_RED_TEAM.md`](07_RED_TEAM.md) — what an adversarial reviewer
   found and what limitations stand.

**For a skeptical reviewer (evidence-first):** start with
[`FINAL_AUDIT.md`](FINAL_AUDIT.md), then
[`07_RED_TEAM.md`](07_RED_TEAM.md) and
[`07_SOURCE_AUDIT.csv`](07_SOURCE_AUDIT.csv), then
[`01_SOURCE_COVERAGE.md`](01_SOURCE_COVERAGE.md) and the ledger, then the
stage-20/30 decisions.

[`00_CONFLICT_LEDGER.md`](00_CONFLICT_LEDGER.md) §C1 at minimum — it
governs how the manuscript may ever be described), and
