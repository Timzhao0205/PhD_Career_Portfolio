# Mission

Produce an evidence-backed decision package for Yiming “Tim” Zhao, a Stanford
EE PhD student in the Senesky group, covering only the following requested
questions:

1. Review at least 150 high-quality, credible, peer-reviewed papers spanning
   the advisor group’s GaN/SiC/wide-bandgap device fields and relevant fusion,
   plasma, stellarator, magnetic-diagnostic, and instrumentation fields.
2. Decide whether the original direction—GaN Hall-effect magnetic diagnostics
   for in-situ magnetic-confinement-fusion measurements—is publishable and
   strategically strong; if not, determine whether to adjust it or change
   direction.
3. Prefer paths that minimize new cleanroom work, reuse established device
   topologies, and place defensible novelty in application, calibration,
   measurement architecture, simulation, data analysis, inverse methods, or
   software.
4. Review the submitted IEEE Sensors Letters manuscript, its source files,
   available HSX data, and the 23-Jul-2026 decision/reviewer comments.
5. Compare a revised Sensors Letters submission against the user’s proposed
   route of an arXiv preprint plus a substantially completed Review of
   Scientific Instruments study using the next HSX campaign.
6. Specify the next experiment and analysis needed to close calibration,
   absolute-field, repeatability, uncertainty, bandwidth, conventional-probe
   comparison, and novelty gaps.
7. Screen only the concepts already supported by the supplied work for
   potentially protectable subject matter before public disclosure. This is a
   research screen, not a legal opinion or a patentability conclusion.
8. Produce a realistic 24-month PhD plan aligned with the goal of graduating
   in about two years and preparing for a startup afterward.

## Fixed preferences and constraints

- Use the supplied files as ground truth; state conflicts explicitly.
- Do not fabricate, infer, or “correct” measured values.
- No new neutron or gamma experiments are in scope.
- Do not broaden the mission into unrelated startup ideas or unrelated PhD
  fields.
- Keep laboratory and cleanroom burden low unless an experiment is essential
  to a publication gate.
- Separate facts, calculations, interpretations, proposals, unknowns, and
  legal questions.
- Make no external submission, disclosure, email, purchase, filing, account
  change, or cloud-document mutation.
- Treat international-student status as a scheduling and career constraint,
  not as a basis for immigration or legal advice.

## Completion condition

The mission is complete only when all stages in `EXECUTION_PLAN.md` pass their
acceptance checks, the 150-paper ledger passes deterministic validation, the
