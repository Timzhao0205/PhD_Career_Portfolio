# Literature and facility seeds

These are discovery seeds, not pre-accepted ledger rows. Independently verify
title, authors, venue, year, DOI, peer-review status, access level, radiation
conditions, and claims before use. Do not cite this file as evidence.

  measurements using Kalman filter for long-duration plasma operations,”
  *Nuclear Fusion* 65 (2025) 046008,
  https://doi.org/10.1088/1741-4326/adb599
- Ivánek et al., “Data fusion of magnetic coils and Hall sensors using Kalman
  filtering,” *Fusion Engineering and Design* 217 (2025) 115180,
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fusengdes.2025.115180
- Quercia et al., “Long term operation of the radiation-hard Hall probes

  sensor,” *Nuclear Fusion* 62 (2022) 106032,
  https://doi.org/10.1088/1741-4326/ac8aad
- Arpaia et al., “Drift-Free Integration in Inductive Magnetic Field
  Measurements Achieved by Kalman Filtering,” *Sensors* 22 (2022) 182,
  https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/22/1/182
- “Integrator Drift Compensation of Magnetic Flux Measurements by Extended
  Kalman Filtering,” *Sensors* 19 (2019) 5455,
  https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/19/24/5455
- Bolshakova et al., “Performance of Hall Sensor-Based Devices for Magnetic
  Field Diagnosis at Fusion Reactors,” *Sensor Letters* (2007),
  https://doi.org/10.1166/sl.2007.004

The 2007 paper is especially important prior art because it reportedly joins
sensing and actuation/self-test ideas. Determine what it actually disclosed
before making a novelty claim.

## Application discovery

- Sandia Z/Mykonos official pages and publications: locate B-dot, current,
  pulsed-power, and AutoMag measurements. Start with
  https://mykonos.sandia.gov/scientific-campaigns/mykonos-power-flow-physics-research/
  and
  https://mykonos.sandia.gov/scientific-campaigns/mykonos-load-physics-research/
- LANL plasma-jet/magneto-inertial-fusion literature: search the LANL
  repository for magnetic probe arrays and plasma-liner experiments.
- ITER magnetic diagnostics and ITPA: use the official pages above.
- PPPL/tokamak/stellarator literature: identify magnetic field/current
  calibration, long-pulse drift, field-error mapping, and alignment needs.
- KSTAR/KFE and the direct 2025 Nuclear Fusion paper: identify current groups
  already working on Hall/coil fusion.
- HTS magnets and rotating machinery: search peer-reviewed work on Hall
  mapping, pulsed-field magnetization, trapped-field rotors, coil-current
  inference, cryogenic sensor behavior, and simpler competing solutions.

Candidate-group information must come from current official institutional
pages and a directly relevant publication. A group is a candidate, not an
endorsement or permission to contact.

## Alternative technologies

Search original/review evidence for:

- standalone semiconductor and metallic Hall sensors;
- inductive/B-dot/Mirnov coils and Rogowski coils;
- fluxgate;
- AMR/GMR/TMR and planar Hall;
- fiber-optic/Faraday current sensors;
- NMR probes;
- SQUID;
- NV-center/quantum magnetometry;
- resonant/MEMS and magnetoelectric sensors where relevant.

Compare only within realistic field, bandwidth, radiation, temperature,
geometry, access, and cost regimes.
