# B70 — corrected synthesis

Resolve every B60 issue or explicitly carry it as a limitation. Produce a
detailed expert report and a plain-language report that agree numerically and
do not hide uncertainty.

Explain:

- what Operation A proved and did not prove about Fable history;
- how blind, old, and new top ideas differ;
- what the peer-reviewed literature establishes, contradicts, and leaves
  unresolved;
- the direction and magnitude of PhD/startup interaction;
- the power-converter/electronics/supply opportunities and missing skills;
- the shared skill stack, bridge experiments, portfolio, and roadmap;
- what the user should do next and which claims require human/professional
  verification.

Required outputs:

- `DETAILED.md`: evidence-rich full report with file and web citations.
- `PLAIN.md`: accessible decision summary, still calibrated.
- `MODEL_REPORT.md`: requested/observed model evidence by stage, effort
  evidence, retries, failures, duration, tokens, cost telemetry, and limitations.
- `SOURCE_INDEX.csv`: deduplicated claim-level source index retaining B15 paper
  IDs, DOI, peer-review status, and limitations when applicable.
- `FINAL_INDEX.md`: exact map of canonical artifacts and reading order.

Pilot: synthesize one representative conclusion from each operation and show
how one red-team issue changes it.
