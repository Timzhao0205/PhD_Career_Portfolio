# Model routing: Hall + coil hybrid/radiation mission

This table applies to
`08_Hall_Coil_Hybrid_Radiation_Strategy_2026-07`, not the earlier folder `06`
mission.

| Stage | Model | Effort | Judgment / budget rationale |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Inventory | Sonnet 5 | high | Deterministic context and requirements extraction |
| Hybrid literature | Sonnet 5 | xhigh | Broad, technical evidence retrieval |
| Radiation literature | Sonnet 5 | xhigh | Condition-sensitive evidence retrieval |
| Applications literature | Sonnet 5 | high | Wide mapping; priority is decided later |
| Evidence merge | Fable 5 | xhigh | Deduplication, source conflict, and prior-art judgment |
| Observability | Fable 5 | xhigh | Central mathematical feasibility decision |
| Radiation compensation | Fable 5 | xhigh | Architecture and uncertainty decisions |
| Applications/collaboration | Sonnet 5 | xhigh | Structured strategic scoring based on validated technical gates |
| Limitations/comparison | Fable 5 | xhigh | Adversarial cross-technology judgment |
| Research program | Fable 5 | xhigh | PhD scope, sequencing, and cost/accuracy tradeoffs |
| Red team | Fable 5 | xhigh | Independent challenge and correction |
| Final synthesis | Fable 5 | xhigh | Final decision and audit |

Seven of the twelve stages use Fable 5/xhigh. Performance and model evidence
are recorded in the mission's `state` directory.
