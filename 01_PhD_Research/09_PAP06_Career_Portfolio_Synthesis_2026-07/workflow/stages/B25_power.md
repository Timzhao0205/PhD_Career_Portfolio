# B25 — specialized power converter/electronics/supply analysis

Inspect the complete relevant old/new/startup pool, B15's accepted literature
evidence, and the underlying publications for consequential claims. Analyze at
least 18 distinct ideas—not only previously selected winners. Include
specialized power converters, power electronics, and power supplies as
products, subsystems, measurement tools, qualification platforms, and
reference designs.

Required technical coverage:

- Hall and coil sensing for ripple, transients, current sharing, ramp/dump,
  protection, fault localization, and calibration;
- converter topologies, gate drive, SiC/GaN/WBG devices, magnetics, insulation,
  EMI/EMC, thermal design, controls, HIL, safety, certification, manufacturing,
  reliability, field service, and supply chain;
- radiation compensation versus bandwidth fusion as separate problems;
- traceability and uncertainty, including why mutual Hall/coil consistency is
  not automatic absolute calibration;
- full end products versus measurement/qualification/reference platforms;
- low-cost bridge experiments with PhD value even if no startup occurs.

Do not imply that magnetic-sensor expertise alone is sufficient to design or
certify a power converter.

Required outputs:

- `POWER_MAP.csv`: at least 18 unique IDs with role, application, PhD leverage,
  missing capabilities, buyer, proof experiment, safety/certification, capital,
  moat, confidence, falsifier, and disposition.
- `POWER.md`: technical/commercial comparison and preferred wedges.
- `POWER_SKILLS.md`: present versus missing skills with acquisition paths.
- `BRIDGE_TESTS.md`: ranked, low-cost experiments with measurements, controls,
  success/kill criteria, cost ranges, safety, PhD value, and startup value.
- `SOURCES.csv`: accepted peer-reviewed technical evidence plus primary
  standards, regulatory, market, and manufacturer sources; include B15 paper
  IDs where applicable.

Pilot: evaluate four distinct architectures/roles and one bridge experiment.
