# B30 — shared skill stack and preparation plan

Synthesize PhD, B15 literature, startup, and power-stage evidence into a skill
acquisition strategy. Distinguish current demonstrated skill, literature-backed
near transfer, missing skill, and skill best obtained through a collaborator or
vendor. Literature prevalence is not proof that the user already has a skill.

Prefer option-preserving capabilities that improve both PhD outcomes and
multiple startup directions. Rank bridge experiments by information gain,
reusability, time, cost, safety, publication value, commercial evidence, and
dependency. Include explicit stop/continue gates.

Required outputs:

- `SKILLS.csv`: skill,category,current_level,evidence,target_level,needed_for,
  acquisition_path,validation_artifact,time,cost,priority,owner.
- `SKILLS.md`: coherent shared-stack explanation and gaps.
- `BRIDGES.json`: ranked experiments with hypotheses, protocols, metrics,
  controls, success/kill gates, dependencies, and dual-use value.
- `PREP_PLAN.md`: 30/90/180/365-day preparation plan.

Pilot: five skills and two bridge experiments spanning sensing and power.
