## Group F — Publication-status conflict (headline finding; full resolution in `00_CONFLICT_LEDGER.md`)

Independently observed, controlling evidence:

1. The parent root `CLAUDE.md` (loaded automatically as project instructions) states: *"2023 — IEEE Sensors Letters (1st author, published)."*
2. `01_PhD_Research_Folder_Info.md` repeats: *"Demonstrated in-situ (2023 Letters)..."*
3. `../05_HSX_ChatGPT_Windows_App/inputs/project/CONTEXT_PRIMER.md` repeats: *"2023 (published, IEEE Sensors Letters)..."*
4. Two files are literally named with "2023" (`../01_Publications/tim_ieee_sensors_letters_GaN_Hall_sensor_in_HSX_2023.pdf` and `../05_HSX_ChatGPT_Windows_App/inputs/reference/2023_IEEE_SensorsLetters_HSX_GaN_Hall.pdf`).
5. **All of the above trace to the same single PDF.** Both "2023"-named files are byte-identical (SHA-256 `e7a990d8215ab88dcd6801b782b6592f76b8cd1a92f28dc982644cab9311d716`) to `../01_Publications/submitted/regular_lsens/regular_lsens.pdf`, whose own embedded metadata shows `CreationDate: Thu Jul 2 14:04:08 2026`.
6. `inputs/Decision_Letter_IEEE_2026-07-23.pdf` is a portal-rendered decision letter dated 23-Jul-2026 for manuscript `SENSL-26-07-RL-1061`, titled "Decision letter (**Initial Submission**)," stating the manuscript "has been reviewed... we must decline the manuscript for publication at this time... invite you to consider submitting a revised manuscript... new Manuscript ID."
7. The manuscript LaTeX source itself (`regular_lsens.tex`) still has unfilled placeholder fields (`Associate Editor:`, `Digital Object Identifier`) consistent with a non-finalized, unpublished manuscript.
8. No DOI, IEEE Xplore record, volume/issue number, or any other independent publication evidence for a 2023 IEEE Sensors Letters paper was found anywhere in the supplied materials.

**Controlling evidence:** items 5-8 (byte-identity, PDF creation metadata, the decision letter, and the unfilled manuscript placeholders) directly and specifically contradict items 1-4 (the repeated but unsourced "2023, published" framing). The "2023" label most plausibly originates from unedited IEEE_lsens template boilerplate in the LaTeX source (`\IEEEpubid{1949-307X \copyright\ 2023 IEEE...}`, line 438 of `regular_lsens.tex` — itself a leftover from the IEEE_lsens.cls example file, not an assigned copyright year) that was then propagated into filenames and, from there, into three independent project-memory documents. This is addressed as the lead item in `00_CONFLICT_LEDGER.md` and is not silently resolved by this inventory — it is resolved there with the evidence stated explicitly per the acceptance checks.
