# arXiv pre-publication scrub checklist

Do not upload until the advisor/coauthors, Stanford OTL as appropriate, sponsor
obligations, and journal preprint policy are cleared.

## IP and content

- Confirm every potentially protectable element has been disclosed to OTL or
  deliberately released with informed authorization.
- Remove unsupported future inventions and confidential facility details.
- Verify figures contain no hidden layers, notes, internal labels, or metadata.
- Review TeX comments for unpublished ideas, collaborator notes, credentials,
  editable links, internal paths, and deleted text.
- Include only files needed to compile the preprint. Do not upload the journal
  submission bundle, cover letter, review correspondence, logs, `.aux`,
  `.synctex.gz`, unrelated graphics, backups, or Git history.

## Rights and submission hygiene

- Confirm all coauthors approve the exact arXiv version.
- Check IEEE's current preprint and copyright-notice requirements for the paper's
  current submission/acceptance status.
- Select an arXiv license deliberately; it is irrevocable for that version.
- Ensure figure and class-file rights permit redistribution.
- Use arXiv-compatible filenames and exact case matches.
- Compile in a clean directory, inspect the generated PDF page by page, and scan
  the upload archive again before the final submit action.

The workflow may draft `outputs/50_SOURCE_SCRUB.md`; it must not create or upload
an arXiv submission without a separate explicit request and all clearances.
