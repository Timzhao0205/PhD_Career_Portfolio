# Questions for an advisor/OTL conversation

## Invention and evidence

1. Is the asserted invention the Hall device, the method of using it in a fusion
   vessel, the GDC-protected package, or a specific combination?
2. What exact problem did the grounded graphite shield solve, and what happened
   before it was added?
3. Are shield dimensions, apertures, grounding points, standoff distances,
   electrical current paths, magnetic perturbation estimates, and thermal paths
   documented?
4. What UHV acceptance criterion was met by EPO-TEK 353ND plus the one-hour
   vacuum bake? Are outgassing, RGA, base-pressure, leak, or bake-cycle records
   available?
5. Was comparable shielding/packaging already used at HSX or another facility?
6. Is there evidence of stable sensitivity, not merely continued functionality,
   across 68 shots?

## Dates and disclosure

7. What are the conception, first drawing, first build, first test, abstract,
   seminar, poster, thesis, manuscript submission, peer-review, and planned
   arXiv dates?
8. Which disclosures were confidential and which reached people without a duty
   of confidentiality?
9. Has the graphical abstract, source ZIP, cover letter, or manuscript appeared
   on any public website or repository?

## People and obligations

10. Who conceived each candidate claim element? Authorship is not inventorship.
11. Did HSX/UW personnel contribute the shield, flange, holder, GDC solution, or
    experimental method?
12. Which Stanford, UW-Madison, SLAC, DOE, NSF, TomKat, facility, material, or
    other agreements funded or enabled each element?
13. Does the PI approve the disclosure, and are inter-institutional ownership or
    sponsor-reporting steps required?

## Commercial value

14. Who would buy or license the narrow package: fusion facilities, UHV plasma
    tools, semiconductor process equipment, or harsh-environment sensor firms?
15. Could a competitor avoid the claim by replacing epoxy, graphite, grounding,
    or the holder? If so, would the remaining scope justify filing cost?

Bring the manuscript, closest references, photographs/drawings, test records,
inventor timeline, and sponsorship numbers. Do not present an AI screen as a
legal conclusion.
