# Stage 10C — applications, alternatives, and group landscape

## Goal

Map where a Hall + coil hybrid could solve a real measurement problem and
where a simpler or different sensor is better. Build evidence for later
collaboration choices without performing outreach.

## Application lanes

- long-pulse tokamak plasma equilibrium/control and reactor diagnostics;
- stellarator field mapping, error-field/alignment verification, and
  coil-current/field validation;
- z-pinch and pulsed-power current/field measurements;
- plasma-jet and magneto-inertial fusion;
- superconducting magnets, HTS field shaping, trapped-field rotors, motors,
  and generators;
- accelerator magnets or other high-field metrology when technically
  transferable.

For each lane determine:

- field magnitude, DC/AC content, bandwidth, pulse/operation duration;
- spatial/installation constraints;
- temperature, radiation, EMI, and survivability;
- current diagnostic and its documented limitation;
- whether Hall + coil provides unique value;
- calibration reference and access to validation.

## Alternative sensor lanes

Collect evidence for standalone Hall, inductive/B-dot/Mirnov, Rogowski,
fluxgate, AMR/GMR/TMR/planar Hall, fiber-optic/Faraday, NMR, SQUID, NV/quantum,
and other credible technology. Do not compare technologies outside their
realistic operating regimes.

## Current group discovery

Use official institutional/facility pages plus directly relevant publications
to identify candidate groups. Record group/lab, facility, current relevant
work, official URL, supporting publication, and uncertainty. Do not infer that
a group will collaborate. Do not collect private contact details or send
anything.

## Outputs

1. `evidence\10C_APPLICATION_SOURCES.csv`
   - exact shared ledger header;
   - at least 40 verified peer-reviewed unique rows.
2. `evidence\10C_APPLICATION_SYNTHESIS.md`
   - application requirement matrix;
   - incumbent diagnostic/limitation matrix;
   - alternative-technology map;
   - official current group landscape;
   - promising and poor-fit cases;
   - source-ID citations.

## Acceptance

- 40 verified peer-reviewed unique sources minimum.
- All named user applications are addressed.
- At least six diagnostic alternatives are evidenced.
- Group status uses current official pages; scientific claims use
  peer-reviewed sources.
- No outreach occurs.
